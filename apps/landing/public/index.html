Player-hosted LAN

Your network. Your race.

  1. HostOne player opens LAN Multiplayer and hosts. The lobby shows the host address and a lobby code.
  2. JoinEveryone else on the same Wi-Fi or wired network enters that address and code.
  3. RacePick cars, ready up, start. Up to 8 drivers. When the host closes the game, the session ends.

Every player needs the same game version. Your firewall may ask whether to allow local network access the first time you host. Internet play, a lobby browser and cross-network matchmaking are not included.

Before you download

Play on your computer

Planned downloads: Windows x64 and Apple silicon Mac. Confirmed compatibility and installation details will accompany each release. Solo play works offline; multiplayer needs computers on the same local network running the same version.

Questions

Where are my saves?

On your computer, in the game's user-data folder. Keep a backup before reinstalling or moving computers.

Can I play with friends over the internet?

Not in this edition. Multiplayer works with computers on the same local network.

Does the game update itself?

No. New versions will be posted on this page. LAN players need matching versions.
