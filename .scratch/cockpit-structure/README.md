# Visible cockpit structure — owner correction, 2026-10-16

The owner reported a ghost car: the cockpit camera hid every exterior surface and left a floating dash/mirror. The replacement is a connected, vehicle-space low-poly cockpit shell: painted front deck and pillars, slim windscreen header/edge, door tops, full-width dash, embedded instruments and mounted mirrors. Every car has a rotating 110-triangle steering rim/hub with its shared-atlas brand badge; bus has a larger, flatter wheel. Bikes retain tank/front fairing, clocks and rotating handlebars. Convertibles have the windscreen frame and no side roof rails. No characters or hands were added. Exterior glass remains dark; glass panes remain invisible from the cockpit.

`TRIANGLES-AND-MASKS.md` covers all 70 configurations. `summary.json` contains the exact final clear-view range and maximum visible interior triangles. All defaults retain at least 80% clear windscreen/road. `tools/cockpit-structure.test.mjs` independently decodes the semantic PNGs, requires rendered structural parts, checks full dash width, verifies steering movement and manufacturer atlas coverage, and rejects ghost/missing-wheel regressions. Existing source-hash and clear-view guards remain active in npm test.
