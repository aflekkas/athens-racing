The incremental CPU baseline is zero (no detail update existed). The benchmark walks 2 km in 0.5 m steps on each map; ring selection only rebuilds after 12 m movement. Off returns immediately. The 240 shared boxes and 96 impostor cards cap added triangles at 3,072 and added draw calls at two. All facade styles share a 1024×512 texture; tree cards share 128×128, together 2.75 MiB including mipmaps. Facades add no triangles or draw calls. Texture painting is generated once per resource activation. Terrain detail reuses existing textures. Base shells remain within the existing 40-triangle guard.

A placement regression found during measurement was corrected: scenery uses the unpinned terrain sampler, so optional decoration cannot change collision-pinned terrain LOD or inflate Off geometry. A regression test enforces that separation.
