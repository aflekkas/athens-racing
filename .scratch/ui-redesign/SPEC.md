# Athens Racing — component direction, round one

**Intent.** Carry the approved title's Greek-road identity into the complete front end. Round one implements Garage, Race setup and Video settings. Every other screen waits for lead/user approval. The shared builders expose an explicit racing appearance so the review release does not silently reskin the rest of the game.

**Palette and surface.** Ink `#0b141a`; slate `#192c35`; white `#f3f6ef`; muted copy `#b4c2c9`; lime `#dcfa4c` for the primary action and selected values. Asphalt panels are flat, with a thin neutral separator. No glass blur, gradients on controls, cyan borders, glow or halo. Red remains a warning/brand colour. Car paint and map imagery supply the other colour.

**Typography.** Screen titles and hero names use bold italic Barlow Condensed, tight spacing and a short lime rule. Values are large condensed numerals. Control labels are clear upright condensed type; sublines use readable Barlow, never faint grey. One decisive headline, one dominant content image and one lime primary action per screen.

**Shared components.** Buttons become compact squared controls with a small corner radius; the primary is a solid lime block with dark text and an arrow. Chips become small rectangular labels. Segmented controls become flat button strips with a lime sliding selected block. Sliders use a lime fill and square thumb; toggles use a square sliding marker and explicit ON/OFF. Selects/listboxes use the same rectangular surface and selected marker. Rows have icon, bold label and useful subline, with an animated lime edge/lift. Cards carry real media above a compact caption and stat strip. Tabs and the settings rail use short numbered labels and a lime selection bar. Stats use caption/value hierarchy rather than nested grey cards. Fields, toasts, back controls and key hints share these corners, spacing and focus states. Focus is a crisp neutral/lime edge, never a glow. Motion is 160–200 ms and respects reduced-motion preferences.

**Screen frame.** The top bar gains a restrained Greek-road coordinate line; Back is a labelled rectangular control. A green Greek/English road sign anchors content heroes. An information strip pairs YOUR NEXT ROAD with READY TO DRIVE. The footer has high-contrast compact keycaps, with gamepad/touch hints retained. Headers, strip and footer are shared builders rather than screen-specific imitations.

**Representative screens.** Garage uses a current-car identity header and a dense grid of actual car thumbnails in saved paint, with cached lazy generation and no boot work. Race setup pairs the actual route image, Greek road sign and large route facts with compact configuration rows and lime CHOOSE CAR. Video settings pairs a concise performance profile/road image panel with a strongly typed settings editor and the redesigned tabs, segmented controls, select and slider/toggle language.

**Acceptance.** Before/after at 1280×720 and 390×844 must be unmistakably different. No native selects or dialogs. Keyboard, gamepad and touch keep their current hooks. No map world or thumbnail renderer at boot; image generation begins only in the Garage. Round-one source, evidence and all required green gates are committed together. Stop for review before extending the appearance to other screens.
