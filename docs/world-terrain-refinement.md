# Hosted terrain refinement

`node tools/world-gen/refine-terrain.mjs MANIFEST_URL OUTPUT [CELL...]` creates a **local development candidate**, without raw OSM/DEM caches, uploads or changes to the running world. Use a new task-owned output directory. The source manifest and every downloaded cell are authenticated by the existing loader and SHA-256 contract. The reduced output retains original cell URLs outside the requested selection.

The height atlas samples the original emitted ground at the global 8 m lattice. A separable nine-tap binomial filter at 32 m spacing removes short DSM/road-profile ripples; steady affine grades remain unchanged and broad hills retain their relief. The correction is evaluated globally before clipping to cells. Render/contact arrays move once, together. The existing 10–22% kerb fade is reapplied when the new broad grade would make an old kerb too steep. Graph points, anchors, signals, vegetation and furniture are resampled against the resulting triangles; buildings remain rigid and follow the minimum supporting footprint elevation.

Authored cells, independent bridge structures, bores and corrected lakes are protected. Their neighbouring 128 m transition stays continuous; unselected cells are protected too, so a reduced build joins its unchanged neighbours. Sea/low shore stays fixed below 2 m, blending into the full correction above 10 m. Protected transitions are **not** evidence of comfortable city streets. The report distinguishes the newly filtered interior, the whole cell, and cells with no eligible road chords. Never infer a whole-world pass from a reduced candidate.

After terrain changes, `conformMarkingSurfaces(pack)` from the infrastructure change clips and drapes paint to its uniquely identified rendered asphalt domain. Different layers fail closed. The authoritative `packTiles` path then checks the original asphalt, shoulder, profile, bridge/rail/clearance, road-section, bore, resident and exact mesh seam gates and recalculates bytes, hashes and triangle counts. No owner limit is relaxed. A separate comfort check measures departure from a 32 m chord, with a 0.5 m limit; it removes only the mathematically affine contribution of a horizontal road bend. A straight roller coaster still fails. An empty eligible sample set is reported as untested.

Before publication, additionally sample **every boundary including changed-to-unchanged neighbours** with `sampleContactSeam`, drive Syntagma/Glyfada/Psychiko in the real game, and inspect matching images and contact/paint alignment. Refresh render variants and world resource/initial download measurements if paint subdivision changes those budgets. The atlas and source URLs preserve the original world data provenance; this creates no third-party image assets.

## Publication handoff

A reduced candidate is not a production release. The parent must integrate the terrain and marking commits, preserve protected infrastructure, and review the full report. Any broader bake must run the same gates over all changed cells and their boundaries. Recompile and re-prove affected route geometry/contact shards, race starts and the eight spaced online grid poses; bind those proofs to the new world revision. Kifissia/Kefalari contact cells and all existing routes require fresh acceptance if included in a later refinement.

Publish immutable cells/manifests to staging only through the established publisher; measure and play the composed candidate there. Before production references it, mirror every dependency to the production bucket and pass the release's manifest-presence guard. Deployment belongs to the parent/release train. No tool here uploads or deploys.

The three sampled Kifisias crossings have correct emitted upper/lower order and pass the existing clearance gate. This does not establish geographic fidelity: the generator's fixed six-metre ground depression and omitted topology require their own source/Street View audit. Smoothing deliberately does not resolve them. Missing asphalt ownership at junction corners is also a separate geometry defect, not repaired by changing heights.
