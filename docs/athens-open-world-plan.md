# Athens Racing: streamed Athens world — research and design

2026-10-15. **Discussion proposal; no product implementation is authorized by this document.** Recommend streaming the existing three maps first, then adding verified connecting corridors through the south suburbs. Treat the Athens basin as the medium-term destination and all Attica as a separate expansion. A larger geographic world need not increase the resident world size; content verification and road geometry costs still grow with coverage.

Today, `loadMap()` fetches the complete schema-1 map JSON and terrain concurrently, constructs one primary `Route`, and begins baked-world preparation. `buildMapWorld()` owns the complete terrain, road network, scenery, traffic, people and disposal lifecycle. Rendering LOD/culling exists but does not bound fetched or decoded data. Roads are already grouped in roughly 300 m cells and terrain in 600 m cells; those groups remain in whole-map packs. The production baker emits gzip-compressed ASR1 typed-array packs for terrain and surrounding roads. A worker-capable cache path can generate and retain **whole-map** buffers in IndexedDB; the bake definition must actually provide a worker URL for it to activate. This is useful infrastructure, not tile streaming.

JSON carries projection, route samples, road widths/speeds, events, buildings, landuse, coast and a version-2 surrounding road list. Terrain has a 32-byte little-endian header, decimetre Int16 heights and Uint8 cover classes. Each map has its own local projection; existing bundles cannot simply be spatially concatenated. The Attica selector is an overview SVG/DEM/vector visualization, not a complete driveable road/building database. Existing network extraction clips to a route corridor, omits motorways from its width table, drops bridges/tunnels/nonzero layers, and only represents `oneway=yes`. It must be replaced for city topology, rather than enlarged unchanged.

### Scope definitions and census method

Downloaded [Geofabrik Greece OSM PBF](https://download.geofabrik.de/europe/greece.html), data timestamp **2026-10-14T20:21:12Z**, on 2026-10-15. File size, SHA-256, exact planning masks, census and derived-extract hashes are retained in `tools/research/athens-open-world/osm-census.json`. `tools/measure-athens-osm.py` reproduces it; the country download and large geometry extracts are intentionally not committed.

**A — Riviera and south suburbs:** explicit polygon encompassing the current Riviera, Vouliagmeni/Varkiza, Vari, Voula, Glyfada, Elliniko/Argyroupoli, Alimos and the coastal approach toward Faliro. It includes undeveloped/sea portions; it is a planning mask, not municipal boundaries. Do not imply Megara or Penteli are connected in this scope.

**B — Athens basin planning envelope:** explicit polygon enclosing the core and the Kifisias/Mesogeion corridors to Attiki Odos, with southern Syngrou/Vouliagmenis approaches and a western Kifisos closure. The named roads do **not** form a closed ring: Kifisias and Mesogeion are radials. The measured polygon is an approximate envelope, not a claim that its edges follow those roads. It excludes most Piraeus port and the Riviera's southern end. The proposed eventual B release is **A union B**, plus a separately scoped and verified Piraeus connection (OAKA lies inside B); rows below measure A and B independently and must not be summed because they overlap. Agree the final envelope before fixing a delivery estimate.

**C — all administrative Attica:** OSM region relation **957716 (GR-I, admin_level=5)**, outer/inner boundary members only, excluding `subarea` members. Includes West/East Attica, Piraeus and the regional islands, including Kythira/Antikythira. It is broader than “continuous mainland driving”: sea gaps require separate island sessions or an explicit ferry/travel transition. No fictional drivable connection. Mainland-first is a viable C subphase but is not the census definition.

Road km are clipped geometric **OSM way kilometres**, summed across motorway/trunk/primary/secondary/tertiary and links, unclassified, residential, living_street, service and road. Dual carriageways count separately; lanes do not multiply length. Footways, cycleways, steps, tracks, paths, proposed/construction roads, ferries and raceways are excluded from the regional census. Restricted ways are reported, not silently offered as public roads; `access`/`motor_vehicle=no/private` is only a lower bound on restrictions. Full conditional/motorcar rules need ingestion later. Distance uses great-circle sums after clipping, not bbox extrapolation. Buildings are `building!=no` footprint entities, with multipolygon relations counted once and their member ways excluded; `building:part` alone is not another building. Centroid assigns boundary-crossing buildings once. Invalid/missing relation counts are exposed in the report. Counts reflect OSM coverage, not a census of all physical buildings.

## 2. Tile streaming architecture

### Grid, geometry and LOD

Use a **512 m square grid**, anchored to one documented Athens world projection, with stable integer `(x,z)` and global OSM IDs. Reproject source maps once offline, preserving primary-route contact geometry and evidence camera positions. Store geometry relative to tile origins. Keep logical coordinates as doubles; rebase the camera/physics origin in 512 m increments before extending toward distant Attica/islands. Fixed-point server coordinates carry a world revision and cell origin.

Make the directed **road graph the first dependency**. A small regional arterial graph supports long-distance routing; 2 km graph shards provide local nodes, edges, lane/width/speed/access/layer attributes, junction connectors, turn restrictions, parking/spawn/tow anchors and references to precise road surfaces. Keep graph topology independently loadable from scenery. Global feature IDs provide ownership: clip road render/contact spans with one seam owner; a building crossing cells has one owner plus neighbour visibility references, so it neither duplicates colliders nor disappears when its centroid cell is offscreen. Every road in the active physics neighbourhood retains full contact detail on every quality preset. “Full detail roads” does not mean triangulating all Athens into memory at startup: far roads remain graph polylines until their surface tiles approach. Quantization of visible meshes must never lower collision accuracy; initially keep contact geometry lossless.

| Ring, measured from player and predicted route | Contents | Initial High preset loading policy |
|---|---|---|
| Safety, approximately 0–750 m | Exact road contacts, junctions, obstacle hulls, fine terrain, verified nearby scenery | Pin every intersecting cell plus a route-ahead safety corridor; no collision LOD downgrade |
| Near, 0–1.5 km | Full roads, simple footprint-extruded buildings, instanced furniture/vegetation | Buildings resolve by distance and visibility; landmarks use bespoke near LOD |
| Middle, 1.5–3 km | Simplified roofs/block masses, coarse terrain, arterial road ribbons | Merge by material and cell; no remote interiors or individual clutter |
| Far, 3–8 km | 2 km parent tiles, skyline/silhouettes and terrain | Aggressive error-based LOD; preserve landmark silhouettes and mountains |

Radii are initial hypotheses subject to byte caps; do not materialize every full-resolution cell in each radius. Prioritize the direction of travel and route branches. At 250 km/h the car moves 69.4 m/s: a 12-second safety lookahead is 833 m, plus the receiving tile. Maintain at least **1 km ahead**, increased to `speed × (p95 fetch+decode+upload + 5 seconds)`. High speed on a poor connection may require a longer graph/surface corridor than the cosmetic rings. Include a rear ring for immediate turns and tow destinations. Nearby multiplayer clients are additional interest centres within the same hard client budget.

### Road-conformed terrain and seams

Offline: acquire one versioned elevation mosaic and vertical datum, derive a global road-height solution, then sample tiles. A shared road profile and junction plane must win over raw DEM. Flatten and grade road corridors with drivable six-metre shoulders using the same contact heights as physics; preserve actual cut/retaining geometry beyond the shoulder. Current tests inspect the **rendered** fine/coarse triangles, not only a height helper: terrain intrusion ≤5 mm, road/shoulder step ≤15 cm, and shoulder slope ≤30° over six metres. Preserve those thresholds for every new road, seam, LOD and transition; target matching shared-edge vertices within 1 mm to prevent an avoidable seam.

A 512 m tile can carry a 33×33 coarse grid at 16 m spacing; add adaptive 1–4 m triangulation around roads and tight cuts. A finer grid is interpolation, not improved DEM survey accuracy. Bake with at least a 64 m halo, enlarged to cover the full conformance filter/structure footprint. Canonical border sample IDs, quantization origins, normal derivatives and road profiles come from the same global solve. Clip render triangles to the canonical boundary and add constrained transition triangles between LOD levels; skirts are a distant visual fallback, never the drivable seam solution. Use a single collision seam owner; renderer overlap must not create duplicate colliders. Road UV distance and lane markings use global edge stations so they do not reset at tile boundaries.

Bridges and tunnels are layered 3D surfaces: conform ground to **ground-level roads**, bridge approaches and tunnel portals; keep the terrain below bridge decks and above tunnel bores. Do not flatten a hill to an underground road or raise ground to a bridge deck. Maintain road-continuous deck/portal/abutment transitions and legal ramp access; use imagery and explicit structure overrides because DEM cannot supply deck/bore heights. The invariant is drivable road/ground contact wherever ground meets a road, with authored grade separation where ground must not meet it.

### Build, decode, activation and eviction

Offline jobs fetch/normalize OSM, restrictions and elevation; grade roads; clip geometry with halos; build contact meshes, routing shards, LODs, procedural building instances, landmarks, texture atlases and evidence manifests. Use deterministic build keys `(source hash, elevation hash, overrides hash, generator version)` and rebuild affected cells plus neighbours. Country ingestion can use a disk-backed node index on the shared VPS. Build and release bounded tile jobs rather than calling today's `generateMapBake()` with a city-sized three.js scene in RAM. Never perform city mesh generation inside a Cloudflare Worker or Durable Object.

At runtime use one decode worker on Potato/Low and two on Medium/High/Ultra. Workers fetch, decompress, meshopt-decode, transcode KTX2, prepare transferables and build spatial indexes. Runtime extrusion is acceptable for bounded background buildings from normalized footprints, never for unbounded raw OSM/DEM processing or unreviewed road grading. Main thread creates three.js objects, uploads buffers and atomically switches a tile from ready to active within **2 ms CPU work/frame** (GPU uploads have a separately measured budget). Workers cannot transfer three.js meshes. Include WASM heaps and KTX2Loader worker limits in the same worker/transient budget; independent loader pools must not each allocate the full allowance. Cap queues and handle cancellation/version races; each job carries world revision and tile generation.

State: absent → requested → decoding → ready → active → retiring → disposed. Validate schema, content hashes, finite coordinates and required neighbours before activation. On corrupt/missing tiles keep the previous tile/parent, retry twice with backoff and expose a non-blocking retry state. Never respond to tile failure by synchronously generating an entire map. If contact data cannot arrive in time, preserve a safe loaded corridor and pause/fade to the last loaded road anchor before the missing region; do not let the car fall through or become blocked by an invisible collision wall.

They are not promises about total process RSS or browser GPU accounting. Measure the existing shell separately and require it plus the world to fit actual test devices. Begin mobile/Low at 750 m near, 1.5 km middle, 4 km far as initial loading radii, independently of the existing preset draw-distance/horizon settings. Preserve those render settings during milestone 1. When caps bind, defer cosmetic detail within the preset LOD policy; if a smaller visible world distance is required, offer an explicit setting rather than silently shrinking it. Never silently reduce contact accuracy or erase landmark silhouettes; show quality tradeoffs in matched views before acceptance.

Evict unpinned far/detailed scenery first using weighted LRU (bytes, distance, view, predicted route and last use), with 20% capacity headroom and 10-second distance hysteresis. Pin collision under cars, live AI, current/checkpoint race corridor, last safe anchor and pending tow landing. Dispose mesh geometries, instanced buffers and nonshared textures/materials; release JS views/parent buffers and cancel obsolete requests. Shared texture/material registries need reference counts. Test 20-minute driving and repeated A→B→A loops for a plateau within 5% of the cap, including context loss and decode cancellation. Eviction must not unload a car's contact surface.

### Formats, caching and request budget

Milestone 1 can retain lossless **ASR1 + gzip** per tile, with a small JSON manifest; it avoids simultaneously introducing tiling and lossy format migration. For later releases use glTF/GLB tile payloads with [EXT_meshopt_compression](https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF/blob/main/extensions/2.0/Vendor/EXT_meshopt_compression/README.md) for render buffers; share KTX2/Basis textures by content hash. Pin decoders to the project's three.js 0.170.0 compatibility and verify actual WebGL formats/transcode memory. Do not assume every device supports one compressed texture format. Keep a separate versioned binary graph/contact/height section with millimetre-scale precision where needed. A range of Int16 height deltas can overflow tall relief/structures: carry a tile height origin and explicit escape to Int32/Float32. Use independent road/contact and scenic payloads so buildings cannot delay driveability.

Use a versioned manifest mapping `(lod,x,z)` to `/world/blobs/<contenthash>.bin`, immutable one-year blob cache headers and a short-lived/revalidated root manifest. Unchanged blobs retain the same URL across revisions; encode schema/local dependency hashes in them rather than a global revision that would change every tile hash. Same-origin static fetches should bypass dynamic Worker routing; today's `run_worker_first:true` must be audited before thousands of tile requests. Do not proxy tile bodies through the DO. Preserve the existing content-addressed cache/release mechanism without eagerly adding all world tiles to a service-worker install list. Cache fetched tiles opportunistically; separate persistent compressed LRU (e.g. 128 MiB mobile, 512 MiB desktop, subject to storage estimates/quota) from RAM/GPU ownership. Offline means cached neighbourhood only. Publish manifest last and retain a complete compatible prior revision during rollout or force a controlled manifest refresh on unavailable old content.

[Workers static assets limits](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers/static-assets/billing-and-limitations/) are **25 MiB per file and 20,000 files on Free**; Paid's 100,000-file limit is not available under the project rule. Target tile payloads **128–512 KiB**, p95 below 1 MiB, hard pipeline cap 4 MiB with adaptive 256/128 m child cells for dense tiles. Always enforce the platform's 25 MiB maximum as well. A per-cell road/scenery/two-LOD design can hit the file-count ceiling long before byte size. C already has **10,664 road/building-occupied cells**: even two files per cell means **21,328 files**, exceeding Free before the existing shell, terrain-only cells, LODs or duplicated content-addressed assets. A and B have 614 and 948 occupied cells respectively; fixed per-cell files are viable there but still need a full deployment inventory. The inspected staging dry run contains **1,336 asset files / 182,780,523 bytes** including content-addressed copies (largest file **19,689,560 bytes**), leaving at most **18,664** files on Free before any further shell growth. Count existing assets and content-addressed copies in every deploy output, then use bounded 2–4 MiB region bundles with an index and HTTP Range only if range caching is proven; otherwise fetch/cache the small whole bundle and extract tile sections in a worker. The public world must fit the current Free asset inventory or its geographic rollout stops; R2/paid hosting is not an assumed escape hatch.

Initial **map payload** budget: manifest + graph + spawn contacts + basic local scenery ≤6 MiB Low, ≤10 MiB High, ≤24 requests; existing car/audio/UI requests are measured separately and cannot be hidden in this number. Warm revisit ≤2 map requests for validation and no bulk world redownload. While driving, target ≤8 new map requests/s (p95 over 10-second windows), ≤0.75 MiB/s sustained Low and ≤1.5 MiB/s High; up to six fetches in flight, no retry storm. At 69.4 m/s, 512 m cells cross in 7.4 seconds; refreshing an idealized 5-cell-wide strip costs about 0.68 cells/s, or 1.36 road+scene requests/s before LOD/prefetch. That is a model, not measured city traffic: record diagonal turns, crossroads, teleports and dense-centre traces. At 10 Mbit/s, Low's steady-state budget leaves headroom; dense tiles must meet it or density/format work precedes expansion.

## 3. Gameplay at city scale

**Traffic and pedestrians.** Spawn from the directed, accessible road/sidewalk graph, weighted by road class, district, time and existing density settings. Use stable `(world revision, cell, entity index)` IDs and deterministic seeds, reserving lanes/positions without appearing in view. Spawn ahead of travel outside view, never in the player's stopping distance or on unloaded surfaces. Maintain logical density in distant cells as counters/seeds, not simulated invisible cars. Initial per-client active caps to profile: Potato 24 vehicles/16 pedestrians; Low 40/24; Medium 64/48; High 96/80; Ultra 128/96. Those caps are solo profiling targets; start online at a common supported physical budget of 24 vehicles/16 pedestrians per interest neighbourhood, with police sharing the vehicle allocation. Higher presets may improve their visuals but cannot add local colliders. Full vehicle simulation near 250 m, reduced update rate to 750 m; pools for meshes, no residents per building. Pedestrians require sidewalk anchors and crossings, never arbitrary terrain points; check layers and keep Megara's track policy. In multiplayer a quality preset may change visuals/update interpolation, but not shared collidable actors.

**Shared traffic authority.** Today one loaded driver owns and broadcasts a route-based full fleet at 5 Hz; world-contact proximity is evaluated with `s,u`. This cannot simply cover separate districts. Initially retain eight-player rooms and one authority per active nearby traffic cluster, elected by the DO; start with one cluster/room and constrain separated traffic until the lease protocol exists. Later use stable spatial-cell leases with a handoff generation/epoch, shared actor IDs and bounded contact reports. Overlapping clusters merge ownership before collisions can happen; disconnected/hibernating owners expire, and authoritative respawn state prevents duplicate cars. DO validates ownership, actor budgets and plausible world positions; it does not simulate the city's vehicles. Distant unloaded shared actors become dormant consistently for every participant. Pedestrians follow the same rule if collidable or affecting traffic; never invent client-specific physical actors online.

**Police.** Patrol and pursuit use graph routes, lawful directional rules, layer-aware visibility, and loaded road contacts. Maintain a bounded pursuit corridor independent of camera LOD; never spawn a blocker in view. Cross-cell pursuit state uses stable IDs and ownership leases. Keep existing time/penalty semantics; add no points, unlocks, economy or progression. Online pursuit actors must be shared; no independent client cops causing contradictory collisions.

**Tow and recovery.** Record safe anchors as `(edge ID, directed distance, lane, layer, world revision)`, not just nearest XY/DEM height or one primary-route `s`. Find a reachable legal edge in the same connected component, outside barriers/junction throats and with room for the whole car; reverse carriageways and bridge/tunnel levels must remain distinct. Load and pin landing surface + neighbours **before** accepting the tow, then place all wheels using the road plane and forward tangent. If that tile is unavailable use the already pinned previous safe anchor. Races keep the server-owned recovery bound before the next checkpoint, so tow cannot skip gates. Test both directions, side roads, seams, tunnels, bridges, towing during eviction, and the first physics step after landing.

**Minimap and GPS.** Build vector minimap tiles offline; use the lightweight graph ahead of scene fetches. Run hierarchical A* in a worker: arterial graph to district portals, then local shards with turn/access/one-way costs; retain a plain Dijkstra reference for route correctness. GPS is a route overlay/instructions from the same edges used by traffic, not a second geometry system. Reroute on actual missed turns and closures with debounce. Report inaccessible/island destinations honestly; no paid routing API. Use global road IDs for saved destinations and invalidate on graph revision changes.

**Races as routes.** Replace the assumption “a map has one route” with a world plus independent route instances. A race is a versioned ordered directed-edge list with offsets, start/finish poses anywhere on validated drivable edges, direction/laps, sector/checkpoint gates and timing rules. “Anywhere” must reject disconnected, restricted, overlapping unsafe grids or unverified roads. Retain current route objects as adapters for the three known races during migration. Generate checkpoint corridors (today gates are at most 80 m apart), bounded gate widths and road heights offline or from trusted graph data; server validates the route hash/revision and ordered gate crossing, not a client-supplied list. The DO receives a compact event corridor, never all city meshes. Race participants prefetch the grid/first corridor; later cells arrive ahead of motion. Free roam has no gates or progression; results remain elapsed times/personal bests only.

**Multiplayer interest and DO load.** Replace route-distance proximity with `(cell, world x,y,z, edge, layer)` interest. At first keep one DO per eight-player room; one enormous Athens DO is not required to share the same static world. Subscribe to a 1 km spatial neighbourhood, enter at 900 m and leave beyond 1,200 m, with a shorter high-rate 250–500 m band and layer/route checks where useful. Relay high-rate nearby cars, slower outer cars, and no distant motion; lobby membership remains visible separately. Bound a subscription to server-known plausible player position, rather than letting a client request the entire city. Tow/teleport sends a discontinuity, destination-load acknowledgement and fresh baseline; drop stale sequence/epoch packets. A future larger public population needs room/zone handoff or separate shards with atomic identity ownership, race-room pinning and reconnection tests; it is not part of the first implementation.

Load model using existing limits: 8 moving drivers ×10 incoming samples/s + 10 world packet messages/s = **90 incoming messages/s**, or **16,200 request-equivalents/hour** at Cloudflare's 20:1 WebSocket ratio. This remains roughly six busy room-hours/day before overhead against the Free 100,000/day allowance. If eight separated traffic clusters each send 10 world messages/s, load becomes **160 messages/s, 28,800 equivalents/hour**; interest filtering reduces fan-out, not these incoming request counts. At 300-byte player samples, 8 players each seeing 3 neighbours costs `8×3×10×300 = 72,000 bytes/s` outgoing player state before world/protocol overhead; all-to-all is 168,000 bytes/s. Batch/delta world state and avoid per-entity messages. No physics, mesh builds or per-frame SQL in the DO; keep current bounded persistence and hibernation behavior.

[Cloudflare DO pricing](https://developers.cloudflare.com/durable-objects/platform/pricing/) also provides 13,000 GB-s/day on Free: continuously active 128 MiB accounting is about 450 GB-s per room-hour, so requests would bind first in the above model; other account usage consumes the same quotas. [The per-object soft limit](https://developers.cloudflare.com/durable-objects/platform/limits/) is approximately 1,000 requests/s, not a promise of low latency at that rate. Profile 8 clustered, 8 separated and later 32 synthetic clients; target p95 handler CPU <5 ms, bounded queues, zero overload responses and actual incoming/outgoing bytes/messages reported. Retain free-plan concurrency limits if measured quotas bind; no implicit paid migration.

## 4. Content and evidence pipeline

1. **Snapshot and normalize.** Download a dated OSM PBF offline, preserve checksum and replication date, coastline, areas, building multipolygons and topology. Keep original node IDs at intersections; two polylines crossing in XY do not connect across layers. Preserve `oneway=-1`, roundabout implicit direction, vehicle/access/conditional rules, turn restrictions including via-way relations, road widths, speed provenance, surfaces, lane turns, bridge/tunnel/covered/layer and private roads. Unknown rules remain marked unknown. Do not reuse the current planar side-road extractor's structure omission.
2. **Elevation.** Reuse known EU-DEM source profiles where valid; ingest a regional raster mosaic for wider coverage rather than millions of rate-limited OpenTopoData point requests. [Copernicus GLO-30 is a DSM](https://dataspace.copernicus.eu/explore-data/data-collections/copernicus-contributing-missions/collections-description/COP-DEM), including buildings/vegetation, not surveyed bare-earth road heights. The generally available free products are 30/90 m; do not assume universal access to EEA-10. Harmonize vertical datum, coast/nodata and raster overlaps. The existing overview's roughly 230 m samples are too coarse for driving. Preserve source resolution and grade override provenance; don't call a 10 m interpolated grid 10 m survey accuracy. Keep [EU-DEM/Terrain Tiles attribution](https://github.com/tilezen/joerd/blob/master/docs/attribution.md) for reused mosaics.
3. **Evidence registry before release.** For each playable corridor require street-level source URL/photo ID, provider/licence, source date, inspected features, actual station coordinates/heading, game pose, source and game image checksums and reviewer status. Start at 250 m stations plus every complex junction, bridge, tunnel portal, narrowing, sharp bend, split carriageway and landmark approach; add stations when a view does not establish the segment. Each released segment references reviewed proof; missing coverage stays pending. OSM/DEM can generate the candidate, but cannot satisfy street-level verification. Every new race/track retains side-by-side proof. Use matched source/game comparison sheets, not synthetic “reference” pictures. Existing Riviera/Penteli source notes and comparison conventions are useful templates; confirm rights for new evidence rather than copying old capture practice blindly.
4. **Imagery rights and coverage.** The project prefers Google Street View inspection, but [Google's current geo guidelines](https://about.google/brand-resource-center/products-and-services/geo-guidelines/) prohibit detached Street View screenshots and downloading imagery for separate use; attribution alone does not authorize it. Use permitted linked/embedded viewing for inspection, and obtain properly licensed own street photos or provider-cleared images for retained side-by-side proof. Do not digitize/trace geometry or build textures from restricted imagery. Verify Mapillary/KartaView coverage, current terms and each photo's reuse rights before adopting either; no API key or blanket coverage is assumed. Preserve attribution and licence records for every retained source image. If permitted proof cannot be obtained, the affected corridor is not release-ready. This may require lead agreement on the evidence workflow, not weakening the rule.
5. **Procedural base.** Extrude actual OSM footprints with known height/levels; mark inferred heights and district facade/roof palettes as estimates. Avoid fictitious landmark placements and generic tall towers. Generate low/mid/far versions from the same seed; remove building holes/parts duplication. Trees, street furniture, signs and parked rows use verified street typology and bounded instancing; do not substitute a random uniform facade wall for an inspected road. Prefer coarse distant block masses before per-building detail. Fine geometry remains reserved for the near view.
6. **Bespoke priorities.** Acropolis: Parthenon/rock and skyline silhouette, with visible approaches and restricted hill access; Syntagma: Parliament, square and road interfaces; Omonia: circle/fountain and surrounding facade masses; Lycabettus: hill profile, summit chapel and visible roads; Piraeus port: terminal/gate/crane silhouettes, quays and actual public-access edges; OAKA: main stadium/velodrome roof silhouettes and approaches. Budget **1–3 person-weeks each** for a first reviewed model/LOD/collider set, subject to source availability, outside the corridor estimate. Use original/compatible-licensed models. The city must also read at distance: prioritize silhouettes before small facade ornaments. Surrounding ordinary buildings remain procedural. Syntagma/Omonia/Acropolis/Lycabettus and OAKA belong to B work; most Piraeus quays lie outside A/B and require an explicitly added connection.
7. **Structure and rule audits.** Build explicit bridge deck/bore geometry and colliders, separate lanes and clearances, portal blending, walls and legal junction connectors. Verify each layer transition and restricted turn in both topology and actual driving. OSM `layer` is ordering, not a height in metres; missing elevations require reconstruction and review. Regression fixtures must include a road beneath a bridge, twin tunnel bores, raised approaches and a one-way loop spanning tiles.
8. **Licensing and reproducibility.** Show linked “© OpenStreetMap contributors” at map entry/credits and on minimap/selector as appropriate, with [ODbL information](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright). Publish the substantial derived OSM road/building database or a compliant alteration mechanism under ODbL when publicly used; attribution alone is insufficient. Separate database obligations from original artwork/code/texture licences and OSM Produced Works. Follow the [OSMF games/attribution guidance](https://osmfoundation.org/wiki/Licence/Attribution_Guidelines), retain DEM/provider credits in matching manifests/CREDITS, and record overrides/source dates. The committed census aggregates/hashes are not a substitute for distributing the eventual playable derived database.

## 5. Shippable roadmap and prototype decisions

**Milestone 1 — stream the existing three maps tile-by-tile instead of whole-map loads (4–6 engineering person-weeks proposed).** Keep maps separately selectable and all existing race/traffic/tow behavior. Start with per-tile baked terrain/roads in the current lossless format, then move buildings/nature/landmarks and dynamic anchors behind spatial ownership. Whole-map lightweight event/graph metadata can remain temporarily, but fetching full map geometry/terrain or generating a whole-map bake is not milestone completion. Preserve existing scenery and proof views at matched poses. Feature flag until green; no geographic expansion yet.

Measure baseline and candidate on the same three starts and mid-route teleports, with the same quality/camera and cold HTTP/IndexedDB/service-worker state. Record bytes/requests, time until controllable car, main-thread blocking, transferred/retained buffer bytes, geometry/texture bytes and JS heap; software GPU times from this VPS are not evidence of GPU wins. Acceptance targets: **≥50% less map-specific startup transfer and ≥40% less resident decoded map geometry/data** versus the whole-map baseline on Riviera/Penteli; Megara must not regress >10% in bytes or time. Low map payload ≤6 MiB and ≤24 requests; cold first drive p95 ≤5 s on 20 Mbit/s/80 ms RTT, ≤10 s on 10 Mbit/s/120 ms RTT; warm p95 ≤2 s. These are targets, not measurements from this research. World caps as above; no new >50 ms main-thread long tasks attributable to decode/activation. Measure 10 cold and 10 warm starts per map. Twenty-minute 250 km/h/reversal/teleport stress must have zero missing-contact frames, no ground seams/tow failures and a memory plateau within 5%. If transfer and targets conflict with identical visual quality, optimize the representation before expansion.

Run existing lifecycle/resource/loading/bake/cache tests; extend real rendered road/terrain and towing invariants across tile seams, eviction and LOD switches. Preserve shader/reflection/water/landmark behavior and per-map custom disposal. Record side-by-side same-pose captures for any rendering migration. Shared resource ownership, current route-based AI/ground sampling and whole-buffer retention are likely the hardest changes.

**Milestone 2 — first connected south-suburb corridor (2–4 engineering person-weeks plus measured content work).** Ship a small, fully verified 10–20 km connector from Riviera toward Glyfada/Elliniko, with directed graph spawning, worker GPS and edge-based tow. This can ship solo while the existing three-map online modes remain available; connected-city multiplayer waits for the ownership/interest gate below. This is the throughput calibration for the region estimate. Demonstrate a trip across old/new boundaries without a loading screen, a race with independently chosen start/finish, one reverse route, one complex junction and network release/version recovery. Publish exact covered roads/evidence, not “Athens complete”.

**Milestone 3 — A, Riviera plus south suburbs (remaining engineering within the A allowance, plus R-based content effort).** Expand in independently shippable district/corridor packs; retain complete street-level proof and road conformance. Add shared-traffic cell ownership and nearby replication before letting online groups separate across districts. Publish coverage manifests; cap unverified road ends at real route boundaries before launch, never in a player's sight while streaming. Reach the agreed A envelope when every intended public corridor has passed the evidence and driving gates.

**Milestone 4 — A union B and city landmarks (an additional 4–8 engineering person-weeks beyond A, plus content/art).** Add basin districts and first Acropolis/Syntagma/Omonia/Lycabettus silhouettes/approaches, then OAKA within B and an explicitly scoped Piraeus connection. Validate dense centre roof/texture memory, urban junctions, tunnels and bridge layers; arbitrary race routes retain trusted gates/timing. Recompute the union census and asset inventory before promising full basin coverage. Rooms stay capped until the interest-management benchmark and free-tier request budgets pass.

**Milestone 5 — mainland Attica corridors, then remaining C (an additional 6–10 engineering person-weeks beyond B, plus content/art).** Connect toward Megara and Penteli along actual verified roads, then East Attica/Sounion and the rest of the mainland. Origin rebasing, larger graph routing, dense/empty tile adaptivity and content-addressed bundle inventory must pass. Administrative islands are explicit optional final packs with travel transitions, never advertised as a continuous car journey. Ship each connected verified district rather than waiting for the entire region. No paid-plan assumptions.

### Prototype first, before committing to implementation

1. **Densest road kilometre:** bake one central Athens junction block, one coastal kilometre, one bridge/tunnel and one building-dense block. Compare current ASR1, indexed/quantized render-only geometry and meshopt; measure exact input/output bytes, decode CPU, transient allocations, full contact fidelity and texture formats. The current Riviera road spike is too expensive to extrapolate blindly.
2. **Two adjacent terrain tiles and an LOD boundary:** deterministic shared profiles/borders and actual uploaded triangle checks, both traffic directions, shoulder re-entry and towing during eviction. Also a layered bridge/tunnel case. If seam/grade ownership fails, stop expansion.
3. **Existing-map route loop:** worker queues, cancellation, reference-counted disposal, byte-based residency and spawn/tow pinning. Prove no hidden whole-map buffer/IndexedDB fallback remains before calling it streamed loading.
4. **10 km proof workflow:** obtain rights-cleared street images and matched game views; time the entire audit. Missing imagery, not extraction speed, may determine the actual release map.
5. **8-player clustered/separated simulation:** compare present relay with cell interest/traffic leases, measuring incoming messages, outgoing bytes, authority handoff, DO CPU and free quota. Larger rooms are a later choice, not a requirement for a bigger geographic map.

Main risks: OSM omissions/incorrect access, sparse building heights, DEM surface bias and vertical datum, graph crossings/layer mistakes, cracks and duplicated collision on seams, mip/normal discontinuities, excessive urban road triangles, draw calls/texture memory, worker decode peaks, stale world versions, cache quota denial, free-plan asset counts/Worker-first requests, traffic lease duplication and server quota. Evidence coverage/rights and manual correction effort can dominate all implementation work. The staged choices above make each risk independently testable.

## 6. Measured offline spike and reproduction

`tools/athens-tile-spike.mjs` builds surrounding OSM road geometry for all three existing maps, assigns whole triangles by centroid to 512 m cells, interleaves the existing attributes as Float32 and measures per-cell gzip level 9. It writes a JSON report only; no tile assets are wired into the game. Uses the source terrain grid, **not the full conformed terrain bake**. Therefore this spike measures partition/byte feasibility and current triangle cost, not final contact surfaces, terrain seams, streaming latency or meshopt quality. Main primary-route/custom circuit geometry, terrain and buildings are excluded from its road payload. Triangles can extend beyond their owning cell: production tiles require true clipping/overlap ownership.

|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| riviera | 47 | 668,662 | 84.17 | 18.53 | 2,225,904 | 54.75 |
| megara | 5 | 10,184 | 1.28 | 0.29 | 178,742 | 2.80 |

`tools/research/athens-open-world/tile-spike.json` preserves exact counts, bytes, per-cell distribution, existing map JSON/terrain sizes and elapsed times. The time includes map/network construction, partition and compression on this shared VPS; it is not browser load/decode timing. No visual change was made and no performance improvement is claimed. There are no new in-game screenshots because there is no game integration.
