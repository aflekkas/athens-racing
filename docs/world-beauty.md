# Athens beauty pass (2026-10-20)

Owner decision 2026-10-20: the open world must feel like Athens, with detail inside the performance budgets. Locked look unchanged: Lighting OFF, Effects OFF, no shadow maps, no post effects. Everything here is unlit vertex colour, shows only at `?map=athens`, and **World detail Off removes it** (the old building boxes come back).

## Step 1: landmarks and the mountain ring

- **Landmarks** (`src/athens-landmark-models.js`, sites in `src/athens-landmark-data.js` from `tools/world-gen/landmarks.py`, layer in `src/athens-landmarks.js`): the Acropolis with Parthenon, Erechtheion, Propylaia and the Athena Nike bastion; the Parliament; the Zappeion; the Panathenaic Stadium; the Temple of Olympian Zeus; Hadrian's Arch; the chapel on Lycabettus. Each is one non-indexed vertex-colour geometry (about 3,800 triangles for all seven), one draw call, placed at its OSM footprint. Walls are shaded from the same fixed NW sun as the facade shader, so they read as lit without a light.
- **Standing on the ground:** `fitHeight` takes the height from the streamed ground. The Parliament and the Zappeion stand level with the street they face (`fit: 'front'`: median of three rows in front of the entrance) on a paved forecourt and steps; the Olympieion, Hadrian and the chapel stand on the highest ground under them, the stadium on the median. Plinths and skirts are buried, so no base floats; a falling street shows a retaining wall, never an underside.
- **Far landmarks:** one beyond the camera's far plane is drawn at 96 % of it, scaled by the same ratio (a perspective camera cannot tell), with a haze that grows with distance, so the Acropolis stays on the skyline above Plaka.
- **Replaced boxes:** the OSM buildings a model replaces (ids in `LANDMARK_HIDDEN`) stay in their cell as hidden meshes, so Off shows them again. Street monuments and fountains inside a landmark's forecourt are skipped by `AthensProps` while detail is on.
- **Mountain ring** (`src/athens-horizon.js`, data `assets/athens/horizon.bin` from `tools/world-gen/horizon.py`, 78 KB): Hymettus, Penteli, Parnitha and Aigaleo. A coarse max-pooled DEM grid; from the camera every azimuth is ray-walked in three distance bands; each band is an opaque strip at the far plane (like the sky), far band first, near ridges over it, coloured greener near and bluer and lighter far, fading into the fog colour at the foot so there is no bottom edge. It rebuilds every 250 m of driving (about 20 ms).

No cell changes, so nothing has to be rebaked for step 1.

## Step 2: polykatoikia and neoclassical character, paved squares

All of it is derived or drawn at run time from what the cells already carry, so **no cell changes and no rebake**; bytes per cell are unchanged (tileJS +0.3 %: the rooftop rows).

- **Facade styles** (`richStyleFor` in `src/world-tile-buildings.js`, painted by `facadeRich` in `src/athens-detail.js`): from the bake's building type and height, a block of three floors and up becomes a *pilotis* house (open, dark ground floor between concrete columns, balcony slabs above), a *continuous-balcony* house (full-width slabs with railing bars, sliding glass doors, striped awnings in green, red, blue and ochre, a shop floor with sign boards) or a *glazed veranda* house. Inside the old quarters (`src/athens-quarters.js`: Plaka, Anafiotika, Monastiraki, Thiseio, Psyrri, Makrygianni, Koukaki, Kolonaki, Exarchia, Neapoli, Metaxourgeio, Pangrati) and wherever a bake stores a historic roof, houses of up to five floors become *neoclassical*: rusticated plinth, tall windows with lintels and sills, open green, brown or grey shutters, string courses, small iron balconies. Split-system air-conditioner units hang on the walls; every wall has a dark foot (baked AO: the contact shadow and grime) and faint rain streaks.
- **Palette:** two bits of every wall vertex's `aFacade.y` (the height field is 10 bits now, 102 m) choose a colour family per building by id hash: white, cream, ochre or pale pink / terracotta (warmer in the old quarters). The shader mixes it into the bake's own wall colour, so the lilac and green variation survives.
- **Pitched roofs:** houses of up to three floors on a rectangular footprint get a hipped roof (about half of the low ones elsewhere, nearly all in the old quarters, a bake's OSM `roof:shape` wins). The ridge vertices carry their rise in the low bits; the vertex shader pulls them down when World detail is Off, so the roof is flat again. Roof faces are tinted terracotta tile in the fragment shader.
- **Rooftop kit:** flat roofs of two floors and up get a solar water heater with its drum tank, a TV antenna and a stair head, one merged instanced model (kind 14 in `src/athens-props.js`, one draw call, 110 m range). Positions are derived with the building meshes (at most 600 per cell, 12 KB).
- **Paved squares** (`src/athens-plazas.js`, data `assets/athens/plazas.json` from `tools/world-gen/plazas.py`, 54 KB, 23 KB gzipped): the 505 OSM squares and pedestrian areas of Attica (place=square, highway=pedestrian + area=yes; not parks, not above 40,000 m2) are marble paving laid over the streamed ground within 330 m of the camera: a 2 m grid of triangles draped on the terrain (4 m for far ones, 6 m for the biggest), joints and a tone per slab in the shader, one mesh, one draw call, at most 30,000 triangles. The Parliament and the Zappeion get a forecourt of the same paving in front of their entrance (no slab wall). The ground bake has no class for a square, so it is plain earth otherwise.
- **Off:** pitched roofs flatten, palette, styles and the dark foot fall back to the plain facade, the rooftop kit and the paving are hidden; monuments and fountains inside a landmark's forecourt come back after a reload (the prop filter runs when a tile is read).

Measured on the 16-cell local bake, same camera, `tools/athens-beauty` scripts (`.scratch/athens-world-beauty-2026-10-20/step2/`), draw calls (detail On / Off): Syntagma 121 / 112 before the pass, 125 / 112 after; Kypseli 128 / 122, 137 / 122; Peristeri 116 / 111, 125 / 111; Glyfada 147 / 142, 149 / 142. Off is the same as before the pass in every view, to the triangle.

## Step 3: Athens vegetation, a denser rooftop kit, the whole Syntagma square paved

Run-time again: no cell changes, no rebake, nothing on the wire.

- **Trees** (`src/athens-trees.js`, rows from `src/world-tile-trees.js`): five low-poly vertex-colour models, 28 to 98 triangles each, drawn instanced within 80 to 190 m: the bitter orange (3 to 5 m, a short trunk and a round crown, dark under-crown and light top), the Aleppo pine with its flat umbrella crown, the cypress flame, the palm and the oleander bush. Each tree has a soft contact shadow, one alpha-blended disc batch for all of them. A species is one draw call when any of it is in range (at most five, plus one for the shadows); at most 420 trees are drawn at once, nearest first; the instance tint varies the green. They replace the box and billboard trees of the shared detail layer (`src/world-detail.js`), which only drew with World detail On anyway, so Off still has no trees.
- **Street trees:** when a tile is read, every quiet edge of its own road graph (at most two lanes, 40 km/h or less, not a bridge or tunnel, on the ground layer) gets a tree every 11 to 17 m on its right kerb, just beyond the carriageway (a two-way street has both directions, so both kerbs): 66 % of the slots in the new quarters, 38 % in the old ones (narrow pavements). A slot is dropped where it falls inside a building, within 4.4 m of another road (a crossing, a junction mouth) or within 5 m of another tree. 88 % are oranges, 6 % palms, 6 % cypresses. At most 700 per cell, 14 KB of rows, derived in the tile worker.
- **Bake rows:** the bake's explicit trees, tree rows and park candidates become pines, cypresses, oleanders and oranges by seed (park candidates lean to pines and cypresses).
- **Rooftop kit, denser:** four small models now (stair head with a tank, solar water heater, TV antenna, pergola with a plant box; `KINDS.rooftop` .. `KINDS.pergola`). A flat roof of two floors and up gets about one heater per 80 m2 of roof (up to eight), an antenna and one more per 250 m2, a stair head on the bigger ones and a pergola on one roof in seven, on a loose grid inside the roof and at least 1.5 m from its edge. At most 1,400 kit pieces per cell (28 KB); 100 to 120 m range; four draw calls in the worst case.
- **Syntagma:** the square's OSM outline is a multipolygon relation tagged `highway=pedestrian` without `area=yes`, which the first plaza extract skipped; it is paved now (`tools/world-gen/plazas.py` takes a pedestrian multipolygon). 569 outlines, 64 KB.
- **Off:** trees, shadows, the kit and the paving are hidden; pitched roofs flatten as before.

Measured on the 16-cell local bake, same camera, the stream settled (resident bytes unchanged for 8 s), `.scratch/athens-world-beauty-2026-10-20/step3/`. Draw calls and triangles, detail On / Off, before step 3 -> after: Syntagma 125 / 112 (84k / 45k) -> 130 / 112 (90k / 45k); Kypseli 137 / 122 (125k / 84k) -> 144 / 122 (147k / 84k); Peristeri 125 / 111 (114k / 78k) -> 131 / 111 (131k / 78k); Glyfada 149 / 142 (130k / 102k) -> 156 / 142 (145k / 102k). The tree layer adds at most six draw calls (five species and the shadows) and 420 trees of 28 to 82 triangles.

## Step 4: fake lighting, no light

The look stays locked (no lights, no shadow maps, no post effects); this step only adds baked-looking cues.

- **Contact shadows** (`src/athens-props.js`): under every kiosk, bus shelter, bench, fountain and monument in range, one soft dark ellipse (an alpha-blended disc, 30 % black, no depth write), all of them one draw call, in the props group so World detail Off hides them. Cars and scooters join them in step 5; the trees already have theirs (step 3). The wall foot of every building (a dark gradient over the lowest 2 m, step 2) is the matching cue for the facades.
- **Aerial perspective** (`FACADE_APPLY` in `src/athens-detail.js`): beyond 180 m, walls and roofs take on the sky's blue and get a little brighter, fully by 720 m, ahead of the fog, so the far city fades into the haze rather than greying. Zero with World detail Off.
- Not done: ground AO along the building bases (the ground shader has no knowledge of the footprints; a skirt strip per wall would add about a third to every cell's building vertices).

Measured as in step 3, draw calls and triangles On / Off, before -> after: Syntagma 130 / 112 (90k / 45k) -> 131 / 112 (90k / 45k); Kypseli 144 / 122 -> 145 / 122; Peristeri 131 / 111 -> 132 / 111; Glyfada 156 / 142 -> 156 / 142. Off is unchanged. No cell bytes change.

## Step 5: street life, parked cars and scooters

Run-time only (no rebake). The bake places about 25 parked cars per cell; Athens kerbs are packed.

- **Parked cars** (`src/world-tile-parking.js`, rows derived in the tile worker like the street trees): along every quiet edge (at most two lanes, 50 km/h or less, ground layer, not a bridge or tunnel) of the tile's own road graph, on its right kerb (a two-way street has both), a car in about two of three 5.3 m slots, a gap after every four to six cars and every ninth slot (a driveway, a door, a bin), none within 12 m of a junction, with any corner of its footprint in a building, within 4 m of another street, within 3.4 m of a bake prop or 2.5 m of a street tree. **The carriageway is measured** from the tile's drivable-asphalt ground triangles (`src/world-tile-asphalt.js`; lane counts are unknown on most edges). Players, traffic and tow anchors use the road up to 2.7 m off the axis (anchors lie 0.5 to 1.7 m off it; some 3 m on boulevards), so a car stands **wholly on the pavement beyond the kerb** (centre w + 1.05 m from the axis) on every street whose carriageway half width w is under 5 m, against the kerb with its inner flank at least 3.2 m from the axis on the 10 m-plus boulevards, and not at all where a building is there or the asphalt is under 1.6 m or over 8.5 m to the right of the axis. On top of that no car or scooter stands within 0.7 m of a 4.5 x 1.9 m player car on **any tow anchor** of the tile (checked box against box). The car (`kind 11`, shared with the bake's parked cars) is a 116-triangle model, 4.3 x 1.8 x 1.4 m: a body with a lower bonnet and boot, a low glasshouse (glass band) under a roof, four wheels, head and tail lights; paint (instance colour) is weighted like the Athens fleet, mostly white, silver and black.
- **Collision:** cars and scooters are walls in the tile's obstacle index (`buildTileObstacleIndex(t, boxes)`, four walls each, up to 1.4 m and 1 m high), so the player and the traffic hit them like a building. Counted in the dressing bytes (about 90 KB per dense cell).
- **Clearance proof** (`tools/athens-parking-proof.mjs`, output in `.scratch/athens-world-beauty-2026-10-20/step5/clearance-proof.txt`): on the 16-cell local bake (8 cells with parking, 721 tow anchors, 1,404 cars and 761 scooters) the smallest gap between a player box on any anchor and any parked box is **0.63 m** (none at 0.5 m or less). Distance of the inner flank from the street axis, and the lane left with a row on each side: roads with a half carriageway under 3 m: 1,477 cars, 1.77 m at the closest (median 2.93 m), 3.5 m of lane at least, and the flank is beyond the asphalt except for 1 car on a bend; 3 to 4 m: 670 cars, 3.27 m, 6.5 m of lane; 4 to 5 m: 3 cars, 4.27 m, 8.5 m; 5 m and up: 15 cars, 3.17 m, 6.3 m (the cars stand against the kerb there).
- **Scooters:** on one slot in eight, a group of two to four scooters stands on the pavement beside the kerb, noses to it (a small model, `KINDS.scooter`, painted by the instance colour like the cars).
- **Budgets:** at most 700 cars and scooters per cell (14 KB of rows, counted in the dressing bytes), at most 300 cars and 260 scooters drawn within 100 m and 80 m, nearest first; both are instanced (two draw calls when in view), and each has its contact shadow from step 4. They collide (see above) and the lanes stay clear of them by construction.
- **Off:** cars, scooters and their shadows are hidden with the props group.
- Not done from the brief: yellow and white kerb paint, manholes and asphalt patches, blue street-name plates with Greek text, shop signs from OSM names, graffiti. They need road-surface decals or a text atlas (a draw call per tile, or a canvas atlas fed from the tile's name strings) and are the next step if the owner wants them.

Measured as in step 3, draw calls and triangles On / Off, before -> after: Syntagma 131 / 112 (90k / 45k) -> 131 / 112 (90k / 45k); Kypseli 145 / 122 (146k / 84k) -> 147 / 122 (150k / 84k); Peristeri 132 / 111 (130k / 78k) -> 134 / 111 (136k / 78k); Glyfada 156 / 142 (144k / 102k) -> 159 / 142 (152k / 102k). ## Step 5b: the Syntagma strip, closed

Two reasons the strip between the paved square and the National Garden lawn stayed sandy, both found by querying OSM along the line:
1. **An unmapped rim.** The pedestrian multipolygon stops at the kerb strip; OSM maps the footway beside it as a line, and between it and the park polygon lies up to 12 m of nothing. `tools/world-gen/plazas.py` now grows every outline by 8 m, minus every lawn, wood and water polygon, and closes a gap of under 14 m between a square and a lawn in full (611 outlines, 97 KB, 40 KB gzipped). At run time the paving skips cells whose ground is drivable asphalt (contact flag bit 0), so a grown outline never covers a carriageway.
2. **Gardens are not a ground class.** The bake colours leisure=park and landuse=grass as grass, but not leisure=garden, flower beds or landscape=lawn, and blends the park's edge over large ground triangles, so a sandy band remains beside any square. A second layer of the same class (`AthensPlazas` with the new `lawnMaterial`, `assets/athens/lawns.json`, 942 outlines, 83 KB, 32 KB gzipped) lays soft-patched grass over every OSM garden of 80 to 40,000 m2 and over the 50 m of park next to a square, on the streamed ground, one more draw call. It also skips asphalt.
- What remains at Syntagma is one footway at the far side of the lawn, which is what OSM has there.
- A shader bug found on the way (the lawn failed to compile because `patch` is a reserved GLSL word) is guarded by a test that scans the layer's shaders for reserved words.
- **Off:** the lawn layer is hidden like the paving.
- **Measured** on a 4-cell local bake of the centre (the only cells the follow-up needed), same camera, stream settled, draw calls and triangles On / Off, before -> after: Syntagma 172 / 155 (207k / 146k) -> 173 / 155 (224k / 146k); Parliament forecourt 168 / 151 (197k / 137k) -> 169 / 151 (217k / 137k). One more draw call, up to 30,000 more triangles (the lawn mesh has its own cap, like the paving); Off is identical to the triangle; resident bytes per cell unchanged (the outlines are app files).

## Step 6a: Greek street-name plates and shop signs

Run-time again (no cell change, no rebake); sites come from the tile worker, text is baked in the browser.

- **Plates:** at every junction of a tile's road graph (three or more distinct neighbours) the two nearest corner walls that face it get a blue plate with white text, about 3 m up, named after the incident street whose axis runs closest to that wall: the OSM `name` of the edge as written (Greek: Αμερικής, Σταδίου, Βασιλέως Γεωργίου Α'), in capitals without accents as Greek signs have them (`signText`), shrunk or broken into two lines to fit. At most 360 per cell, 0.9 to 1.6 m wide, 4:1.
- **Shop signs:** one site per building, the road-facing wall with the best length-to-distance score, for buildings of 6.5 m and up with a ground-floor facade style (shops, balconies, awnings, neoclassical, veranda; not the open pilotis floor). A site takes the name of an OSM shop or amenity within 15 m (`assets/athens/pois.json`, from `tools/world-gen/pois.py`, kinds: pharmacy, cafe, bakery, kiosk, supermarket, restaurant, bank, hairdresser, clothes) and otherwise, on 40 to 88 % of the sites by facade style, a generic Greek sign: ΦΑΡΜΑΚΕΙΟ with the green cross, ΠΕΡΙΠΤΕΡΟ (green), ΚΑΦΕ, ΦΟΥΡΝΟΣ, ΨΗΤΟΠΩΛΕΙΟ, ΤΑΒΕΡΝΑ, ΣΟΥΠΕΡ ΜΑΡΚΕΤ, ΚΟΜΜΩΤΗΡΙΟ, ΕΝΔΥΜΑΤΑ and a dozen more, each kind with its own palette. The OSM extract of the cache is filtered (24 shop nodes, 951 named shops and amenities in all), so almost every sign is generic until a fuller extract is run through `pois.py` (HANDOVER).
- **Atlas, nothing shipped:** one canvas atlas, 2048 x 1024 px of 256 x 64 px slots (128 signs, 11 MB on the GPU with mips, not counted in the 192 MiB admission figure), each sign painted the first time it comes within range and kept while it stays in range; the nearest signs keep their slots, the farther ones wait their turn. Sign quads are instanced (one draw call), unlit, drawn within 75 m, mip bias -0.4, anisotropy 4, and dissolve by an interleaved-gradient dither over the last 16 m, so the text is sharp at street level and gone before it can shimmer.

## Step 5d: cars sit on the ground, with wheels showing

The owner's complaint: parked cars looked squatted, crushed and sunk, bunched nose to tail and straddling the kerb. Cause: the rows took the height of the edge axis (the road centre), while the pavement or kerb they stand on is higher and sloped, so 1,401 of 1,648 cars in the four cells round the Glyfada view were sunk, up to 0.63 m.
- **Ground under the wheels** (`src/world-tile-ground.js`, `groundIndex`): a height lookup over the cell's fine and contact triangles (8 m grid, barycentric), no new data. Every car is placed car by car along the kerb; the four wheel points are looked up and the car sits on the highest, so none sinks (floor 17 cm up on its wheels).
- **No straddling:** a car is dropped when the ground under its wheels differs by more than 15 cm across the car (a kerb step between the near and far wheels) or more than 40 cm along it (a slope), so a car is wholly on the road or wholly on the pavement. Heading and centre are fitted to the measured kerb line at both car ends, so cars follow a bend instead of fanning out.
- **Gaps:** at least 0.8 m between the oriented boxes of neighbouring cars (variable pitch), runs of 5 to 10 cars end in an empty bay, scooters keep 0.3 m from cars. Rows still stop at junctions and driveways (the existing clearance rules), and the median exclusion of step 5c stands.
- **Model** (`src/athens-props.js`, 116 triangles): 1.92 x 4.30 m, 1.43 m high with its 0.17 m sill, 0.62 m wheels (visible), cabin glass band, bonnet and boot steps, lights; checked in a test (`car size`) and in the close-up shots.
- **Result** on the east cells 3,21 3,22 2,21 4,21: 1,699 cars, none sunk, none overlapping, none closer than 0.8 m, none with a split lateral wheel height. Clearance proof (`tools/athens-parking-proof.mjs`, the same eight cells as 5c): 798 anchors, 3,058 cars and 1,828 scooters, smallest gap to a 4.5 x 1.9 m player box **0.70 m**, none at 0.5 m or less. Draw calls, triangles per car (116) and bytes per cell are unchanged in kind (no new layer; rows are the same size), Off removes them as before.

## Step 5e: cars that read as cars at 20 to 60 m

## Owner fix wave: sparse, whole parked cars and contained road marks (2026-10-22)

The reproducible distortion was scratch-state leakage: `AthensProps.rebuild` composed prop matrices with the previous contact shadow's scale. Staging cars measured 1.9 by 1.1 or 1.2 by 2.6 in the horizontal axes. Resetting to unit scale before every prop pass preserves the authored car silhouette through movement and tile replacement; these meshes have no LOD substitution and already disable frustum culling.

`PARKING_DENSITY` in `src/world-tile-parking.js` is the single occupancy control (0.18), also used by Psychiko's surveyed parking. Candidates leave 9 m empty steps, start at least 16 m from junction ends, skip arterial speeds/lane counts, buildings, other road mouths, trees and tow anchors. Cars require a complete asphalt footprint with kerb clearance and a 3.2 m driving corridor, at least 2 m between boxes, and at most 8 cm of height difference under the wheels. Narrow roads no longer substitute pavement parking. Unsafe old baked prop cars are hidden; the validated derived rows own cars and collisions. The tile worker removes candidates within 17 m of a bus stop/shelter or 11 m of a crossing before building colliders. Untagged driveways can only be inferred from road mouths and empty runs.

Classic asphalt rows store surface ID at offset 3 and flags at offset 4; using the former as flags misclassified some paving. The lookup now reads the flags and excludes decks. Road marks validate their entire footprint, inset kerb paint beyond the reach query's 12.5 cm uncertainty, generate one repair set per two-way road, and drape every emitted vertex on streamed road contact with a 2.5 cm depth gap. This changes runtime dressing only; no world assets or R2 data are rebaked.

Regression checks cover repeated prop rebuilds, sparse spacing, whole asphalt/support footprints, wall/anchor/stop/crossing clearance, classic contact flags, and sloped/contained decals.
