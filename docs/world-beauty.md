# Athens beauty pass (2026-10-20)

Owner decision 2026-10-20: the open world must feel like Athens, with detail inside the performance budgets. Locked look unchanged: Lighting OFF, Effects OFF, no shadow maps, no post effects. Everything here is unlit vertex colour, shows only at `?map=athens`, and **World detail Off removes it** (the old building boxes come back).

## Step 1: landmarks and the mountain ring

- **Landmarks** (`src/athens-landmark-models.js`, sites in `src/athens-landmark-data.js` from `tools/world-gen/landmarks.py`, layer in `src/athens-landmarks.js`): the Acropolis with Parthenon, Erechtheion, Propylaia and the Athena Nike bastion; the Parliament; the Zappeion; the Panathenaic Stadium; the Temple of Olympian Zeus; Hadrian's Arch; the chapel on Lycabettus. Each is one non-indexed vertex-colour geometry (about 3,800 triangles for all seven), one draw call, placed at its OSM footprint. Walls are shaded from the same fixed NW sun as the facade shader, so they read as lit without a light.
- **Standing on the ground:** `fitHeight` takes the height from the streamed ground. The Parliament and the Zappeion stand level with the street they face (`fit: 'front'`: median of three rows in front of the entrance) on a paved forecourt and steps; the Olympieion, Hadrian and the chapel stand on the highest ground under them, the stadium on the median. Plinths and skirts are buried, so no base floats; a falling street shows a retaining wall, never an underside.
- **Far landmarks:** one beyond the camera's far plane is drawn at 96 % of it, scaled by the same ratio (a perspective camera cannot tell), with a haze that grows with distance, so the Acropolis stays on the skyline above Plaka.
- **Replaced boxes:** the OSM buildings a model replaces (ids in `LANDMARK_HIDDEN`) stay in their cell as hidden meshes, so Off shows them again. Street monuments and fountains inside a landmark's forecourt are skipped by `AthensProps` while detail is on.
- **Mountain ring** (`src/athens-horizon.js`, data `assets/athens/horizon.bin` from `tools/world-gen/horizon.py`, 78 KB): Hymettus, Penteli, Parnitha and Aigaleo. A coarse max-pooled DEM grid; from the camera every azimuth is ray-walked in three distance bands; each band is an opaque strip at the far plane (like the sky), far band first, near ridges over it, coloured greener near and bluer and lighter far, fading into the fog colour at the foot so there is no bottom edge. It rebuilds every 250 m of driving (about 20 ms).

No cell changes, so nothing has to be rebaked for step 1.

## Step 2: polykatoikia and neoclassical character, paved squares

All of it is derived or drawn at run time from what the cells already carry, so **no cell changes and no rebake**; bytes per cell are unchanged (tileJS +0.3 %: the rooftop rows).

- **Facade styles** (`richStyleFor` in `src/world-tile-buildings.js`, painted by `facadeRich` in `src/athens-detail.js`): from the bake's building type and height, a block of three floors and up becomes a *pilotis* house (open, dark ground floor between concrete columns, balcony slabs above), a *continuous-balcony* house (full-width slabs with railing bars, sliding glass doors, striped awnings in green, red, blue and ochre, a shop floor with sign boards) or a *glazed veranda* house. Inside the old quarters (`src/athens-quarters.js`: Plaka, Anafiotika, Monastiraki, Thiseio, Psyrri, Makrygianni, Koukaki, Kolonaki, Exarchia, Neapoli, Metaxourgeio, Pangrati) and wherever a bake stores a historic roof, houses of up to five floors become *neoclassical*: rusticated plinth, tall windows with lintels and sills, open green, brown or grey shutters, string courses, small iron balconies. Split-system air-conditioner units hang on the walls; every wall has a dark foot (baked AO: the contact shadow and grime) and faint rain streaks.
- **Palette:** two bits of every wall vertex's `aFacade.y` (the height field is 10 bits now, 102 m) choose a colour family per building by id hash: white, cream, ochre or pale pink / terracotta (warmer in the old quarters). The shader mixes it into the bake's own wall colour, so the lilac and green variation survives.
- **Pitched roofs:** houses of up to three floors on a rectangular footprint get a hipped roof (about half of the low ones elsewhere, nearly all in the old quarters, a bake's OSM `roof:shape` wins). The ridge vertices carry their rise in the low bits; the vertex shader pulls them down when World detail is Off, so the roof is flat again. Roof faces are tinted terracotta tile in the fragment shader.
- **Rooftop kit:** flat roofs of two floors and up get a solar water heater with its drum tank, a TV antenna and a stair head, one merged instanced model (kind 14 in `src/athens-props.js`, one draw call, 110 m range). Positions are derived with the building meshes (at most 600 per cell, 12 KB).
- **Paved squares** (`src/athens-plazas.js`, data `assets/athens/plazas.json` from `tools/world-gen/plazas.py`, 54 KB, 23 KB gzipped): the 505 OSM squares and pedestrian areas of Attica (place=square, highway=pedestrian + area=yes; not parks, not above 40,000 m2) are marble paving laid over the streamed ground within 330 m of the camera: a 2 m grid of triangles draped on the terrain (4 m for far ones, 6 m for the biggest), joints and a tone per slab in the shader, one mesh, one draw call, at most 30,000 triangles. The Parliament and the Zappeion get a forecourt of the same paving in front of their entrance (no slab wall). The ground bake has no class for a square, so it is plain earth otherwise.
- **Off:** pitched roofs flatten, palette, styles and the dark foot fall back to the plain facade, the rooftop kit and the paving are hidden; monuments and fountains inside a landmark's forecourt come back after a reload (the prop filter runs when a tile is read).

Measured on the 16-cell local bake, same camera, `tools/athens-beauty` scripts (`.scratch/athens-world-beauty-2026-10-20/step2/`), draw calls (detail On / Off): Syntagma 121 / 112 before the pass, 125 / 112 after; Kypseli 128 / 122, 137 / 122; Peristeri 116 / 111, 125 / 111; Glyfada 147 / 142, 149 / 142. Off is the same as before the pass in every view, to the triangle.
