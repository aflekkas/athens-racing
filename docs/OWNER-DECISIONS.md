The invitation is a small, dismissible **kit notice** (`C.notice`) on the **title screen only**, on phones and tablets only, never inside the installed app (display-mode standalone/fullscreen or `navigator.standalone`), never over the sign-in dialog and never during driving. It is not a dialog (sign-in stays the only one). Android/Chrome captures `beforeinstallprompt` and offers INSTALL; iOS shows SHARE then ADD TO HOME SCREEN; other phone browsers get their menu steps. It shows after a finished race or a few launches, not to a brand-new visitor. NOT NOW (or the close button) hides it for 7 days, DON'T ASK AGAIN for good, and installing for good, all in localStorage (`paraliaki.install.v1`). Lucide icons only, no coloured focus outline, no hover effects, animated in and out. Logic in `src/install-prompt.js`; guards: `tools/owner-locked-install-prompt.test.mjs`, `tools/install-prompt.test.mjs`, `tools/web-manifest.test.mjs`.
