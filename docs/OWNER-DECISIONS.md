# Owner-locked decisions

These choices were made deliberately by the owner (Alexandros Lekkas). Don't change, revert or "improve" any of them without asking the owner first. Guard tests enforce several of them, and their failure messages point here. Add new entries at the bottom, dated, in the owner's words where possible.

Vehicle geometry limits live in `src/vehicle-budgets.js` and are enforced for every catalogue configuration by `tools/vehicle-budgets.test.mjs`, included in `npm test`. Counts include rendered wheels and exterior attachments: player exterior at most 4,000 triangles (bus/truck 5,000), LOD1 below 1,000, LOD2 below 300, each spinning wheel below 150, cockpit below 3,000, bike rider below 600. Traffic near/far models follow the same exterior/LOD2 ceilings. Enclosed vehicle occupants and visible cockpit glass must both be zero. Existing wheel positions/radii, dimensions and exhaust anchors stay intact. The badge atlas remains decals. The guards reject nonfinite geometry and check glass visibility through repeated camera switches.

Do not raise these limits or restore occupants/tinted cockpit glass without the owner's decision. Guard failures must say: **Owner-locked decision, see docs/OWNER-DECISIONS.md. Ask the owner before changing.**

2026-10-16 owner correction: **Cockpits must not be ghost cars.** This supersedes the floating thin-dash / blanket own-exterior hiding interpretation above. Every cockpit must show its own connected low-poly dash, inset instruments, bonnet/front deck, windscreen pillars and header/edge, door tops/window frames, and mounted mirrors. Convertibles retain their windscreen frame without a roof; bikes show tank/front fairing, bars and clocks. The lightweight inner shell is visible only in POV. Invisible glass and no occupants/hands remain locked. **At least 80% clear windscreen/road remains mandatory**, with all structure and steering counted against the remaining 20%. `tools/cockpit-structure.test.mjs` independently recounts rendered part-ID pixels for all 70 configurations and rejects missing/hidden/off-screen structure; `tools/pov-owner-lock.test.mjs` retains the clear-view and source-hash guard.

2026-10-16 owner addition: **“We need to be able to see the steering wheel.”** Every car's default POV has a visible blocky rim, hub and manufacturer badge from the shared atlas; the wheel rotates with actual steering input. Bus has a larger, flatter wheel; bikes have rotating handlebars. No hands or arms. The cockpit structure guard checks rendered rim/hub/badge pixels and steering movement across all 70 configurations.

2026-10-16 owner rain correction: **“I hope raindrops and puddles are really a texture overlay rather than real particles.”** Windscreen rain is one mesh sampling one shared 128px tiled alpha droplet texture, with small sparse drops and a wipe-mask uniform. No per-drop meshes, sprites, particles or rain-lens distortion. Wiper pivots attach to the cowl and blades sweep on the framed windscreen plane; the same sweep drives the alpha mask. World rain/puddles remain owned by the separate world-weather pass. Guarded by `tools/cockpit-structure.test.mjs`; same-pose rain before/after and draw-call evidence are in `.scratch/cockpit-structure/`.
