PARALIAKI RACING — ENGINE RECORDINGS

These are edited recordings of real engines. They are not all exact recordings
of the represented car. sources.json records every source, trim, estimated RPM,
license URL, download URL, transformation and SHA-256 checksum.

IMPLEMENTATION AND LIMITATIONS
The public Freesound HQ MP3 previews were used, not the account-gated original
WAVs. Sonniss sources are original 96 kHz WAVs. Runtime files are PCM16 mono
44.1 kHz WAV: no decoder padding at seams, and no external audio requests.
RPM references are harmonic-analysis estimates, except the producer-labelled
TDI loops. Short sections of revs were pitch-stabilized, seams overlapped, DC
removed and levels matched. The high MINI loop is short (0.43 s); a longer
steady full-load recording would improve variation. Load uses filtering and
gain, not separate measured load/coast recordings. World sounds have their own
credits; turbo/shift effects are procedural. The existing rival shares the
player's performance/audio bank. Engine synthesis remains as a decode fallback.

ADDITIONAL SYNTHESIZED ENGINES
Porsche 991.1 Carrera 3.4: flat6, six-cylinder pulse model, 7,800 RPM redline.
Toyota GR Yaris 1.6: turbo3, three-cylinder pulse model, 7,200 RPM redline.
Alfa Romeo 2000 GTV: na4, four-cylinder twin-cam character, 6,500 RPM redline.
These are procedural approximations, not recordings of the named cars. Each
has four RPM bands with load/coast layers. The Yaris uses turbo spool/lift-off
effects; Carrera and GTV do not. No recorded startups are assigned to these cars.

Audition all six: /tools/audio-preview.html (served from the project root).
Rebuild: python tools/build_engine_audio.py --cache /path/to/source-cache
For just the MINI mid layer, append --only mini-mid.wav. Its current trim is
76–78 seconds of the same CC0 Cooper S recording, with the existing pitch
stabilization and loop crossfade. This replaces a more uneven 29–32 second
section; the original edited WAV is preserved as mini-mid.wav.orig and its
trim/checksum are in sources.json (previousVersion). No EQ or envelope
normalization was applied. The idle and other RPM layers are unchanged.
Requires ffmpeg, numpy and scipy. Masters are cached outside runtime assets.
To upgrade fidelity further, replace substitute sources with separately
licensed Z4 B58 / Octavia EA288 / MINI B48 ignition recordings, plus sustained
on-load and off-load dyno RPM takes. Do not use game-ripped mod audio as masters.

Sound v2 runtime playback: MINI layers and other takes shorter than 1.25 seconds
use a cached 24-second granular bed. Random source offsets and +/-40% onset
jitter retain varied texture. Sine equal-power windows overlap by at least 60%.
The recorded firing body is phase-aligned and divided by the window sum;
decorrelated texture is divided by the square root of summed window powers.
Each summed band receives smooth look-ahead RMS normalization. Circular
overlap-add keeps the long-bed wrap continuous. Audio-rate pitch follows RPM
and Doppler. No source audio asset or licence changed.
