ATHENS STREET RACERS — CONTACT-PATCH AUDIO (sound v2)

Rolling, scrub, kerb rumble, grit, stick/slip resonances and lock-up chirps:
original procedural synthesis implemented in src/audio.js (TyreVoice).
No external recordings are used for these generated components; they follow
this repository's source-code licence. No claim of a new third-party CC licence.

Recorded rubber texture reuses ../world/tyre-squeal.mp3:
"Chrysler LHS tire squeal 04 (04-25-2009).wav" by audible-edge
https://freesound.org/people/audible-edge/sounds/71739/
CC0 1.0: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
Existing asset trimmed 3.6–14.2 s, high-passed at 300 Hz, crossfaded as a loop.
Sound v2 plays decorrelated, pitch-shifted copies per wheel, blended with
procedural resonances; volume follows normal load and combined tyre slip.
Full acquisition provenance and checksums: ../world/sources.json.

Audition renders also contain existing engine/world/music recordings; see
../engines/CREDITS.txt, ../world/CREDITS.txt and ../music/CREDITS.txt.
Reproduce locally: node tools/build_sound_audio.mjs, open http://localhost:8015.
WAVs, spectrograms and EBU R128 measurements are written to .scratch/sound-v2/.

Grip progression: band-limited noise/howl around 450–700 Hz grows through
65–95% of peak combined slip, receding as squeal builds progressively above peak.
A 1.2 kHz low-pass keeps the scrub soft and below the squeal modes.
Squeal uses six detuned narrow noise resonances per wheel, covering a rising
fundamental and second/third harmonics. Random 4–12 Hz pitch motion (+/-3.8%),
amplitude flutter and short irregular stick/slip dips roughen the modes; there
are no sustained sine tones. Each wheel excites these modes from an independent
19-second noise source with a random starting offset and a drifting playback rate.
The CC0 rubber recording stays underneath as texture. No new recordings added.

corner-sweep.wav/png sweeps normalized slip 0–150% over eight seconds.
Call the exported renderTyreReview() for all sweep/solo pairs, or
renderSoundV2({scenarios:['corner-sweep'], isolate:layer}) for solo WAV/PNG
pairs; layer can be tyre-roll, tyre-scrub, tyre-squeal, tyre-recording, wind,
engine, engine-low or engine-mid. The tyres option includes all wheel/wind layers.
Use suffix:'before' to retain a diagnostic comparison. Output goes to
.scratch/sound-v2/. For WAV signal regression, run
python tools/build_sound_audio_analysis.py (numpy/scipy).
