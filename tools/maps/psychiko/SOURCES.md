# Psychiko sources and reproduction

Revision: 2026-10-19, **Faros Circuit v3**. The 5,732.284 m fictional closed-road lap starts and finishes immediately before the Faros signal turn into 25is Martiou. It follows the reviewed public OSM surface ways, in their legal directions. The race is two laps with two rivals; the disabled time trial is one lap. Reverse racing stays disabled in solo, events and multiplayer. All six raced roundabouts circulate counter-clockwise. A/B squares retain their complete rings, islands and approach connectors, including the arcs the timed line does not use. Roadworks close outgoing side streets in races and disappear in free roam.

The exact ordered ways, turn decisions, profile, matched imagery, performance and gates are in [implementation D](../../../.scratch/psychiko-realism/impl-d/README.md). `plan-faros.mjs` restricts each named leg to explicitly reviewed OSM way IDs, respects one-way tags, and freezes the resulting edge list. `route.mjs` retains that provenance while filleting/sampling the road centreline. The start offset is -16 m from node 1285491927, on the incoming surface approach, so successive laps share one timing line.

### Street-name and surface decisions

- Faros enters Psychiko on 25is Martiou, ways 465224134 / 661584002 / 62331516, then A square 25053095. Kifisias itself passes underneath here: the lap stays on the real at-grade signal/frontage junction rather than inventing a crossing of the tunnel lanes.
- Diamantidou passes B square 25053533 (named Eleftheriou Venizelou in OSM) and reaches the Eleftherias rim before the right turn onto Nikiforou Lytra. The rim remains CCW.
- Achillea Paraschou 97722998 ends at Dryadon. Dryadon 97723002 and Pavlou Mela 31486851 supply the real 101 m connection to Davaki; there is no direct Paraschou–Davaki junction in this extract.
- Bizaniou 97723004 continues through the short Korytsas 31270138 connection onto Emmanouil Benaki 545646475. The College wall is on the driver's left after the sharp right.
- The requested “Arsaki” is mapped as Αρεακη (Areaki), way 25053494; it is the described right turn from Benaki to Michalakopoulou square 634346640. Ethnarchou Makariou 107941912 is a one-way southeast departure from that square, away from Malakasi. The lap circulates CCW directly to the west Malakasi exit; it does not take an invented Makariou-to-Malakasi connector.
- Left from Malakasi onto Stratigou Kallari keeps the park on the left. Left onto Elikonos reaches the light and right onto the legal southbound Kifisias carriageway: 662139350 / 216905683 / 664440151 / 465205389, then surface frontage 476108293 / 113339579 / 371561413 to Faros. The frontage fork avoids the underpass.
- Reviewed square connectors 661213888 / 661217861 / 661217863 / 661542256 / 661584000 / 661583998 / 978004330 remain open with the rings. None is driven backwards. Other outgoing arms are closed beyond the raced pavement.

Free roam adds both at-grade Kifisias directions throughout the Psychiko frontage and retains every new course street outside the municipality. The selected network has 672 road parts / 86.292 km plus the primary route. The explicitly omitted tunnel ways are 25117418, 25117422, 113339568 and 113339581. No bridge, tunnel or nonzero-layer way is used by the race. Kifisias uses the existing T10/D8/F5 sections according to frozen OSM lane counts; those are OSM-derived estimates, separate from the Street View survey. Dashed lane dividers, low-cost median pieces, arterial lamps and Faros/Elikonos signals share existing material/cell meshes. Signals and median placement are approximate scenery, not a reconstructed traffic-control simulation.

Old personal records use `.garden-v2`; new records use `.faros-v3`. Native and Worker boards add `:faros-v3` to the route version. Ghosts and lobbies already compare the complete route fingerprint; the former fingerprint `653bca47` cannot match this course. Older records stay stored.

## Frozen inputs

- `raw/osm.json.gz`: original 2026-10-14 primary [Overpass](https://overpass-api.de/api/interpreter) response for south 37.992 / west 23.758 / north 38.027 / east 23.799. Nodes, ways and source IDs are retained. © [OpenStreetMap contributors](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright), ODbL 1.0.
- `raw/boundaries.json`: Overpass response with municipal relation [1403235](https://www.openstreetmap.org/relation/1403235) for Palaio Psychiko. The public motor-road network inside the municipality, the area enclosed by the lap and ways near the lap are selected. Public-road selection excludes private/no motor access, private driveways and non-motor paths.
- `raw/drawn-course.json`: explicit OSM nodes, source way per edge, reviewed internal ring connectors and startOffset. `plan-faros.mjs` searches only the reviewed named-way list for each leg; `route.mjs` verifies adjacency/public paving, legalizes every ring and samples the filleted centreline at approximately 2 m. The timing line is 16 m before the first node on the Faros surface approach. Narrow mapped junctions can have a 3.6 m minimum radius; no street/block shortcut is added. Elevation stations are regenerated from this new XY path.

- `raw/copernicus.json.gz` (54 KB): [Copernicus DEM GLO-30 Public, 2021 release](https://copernicus-dem-30m.s3.amazonaws.com/readme.html), range-read from the public AWS COGs over the whole terrain bbox plus 0.002° (`fetch_copernicus.py`, 2026-10-19; windows of tiles N37/N38 E023, integer centimetres, EGM2008). © DLR e.V. 2010-2014 and © Airbus Defence and Space GmbH 2014-2018 provided under COPERNICUS by the European Union and ESA. It is a surface model (~30 m), not a surveyed bare-earth DTM. `elevation.mjs` mosaics the windows, applies one **sigma-20 m spatial Gaussian** and fits each complete roundabout ring with one least-squares plane along its carriageway (blended back over 20 m). That single field gives the lap, every side road, junction, ring and the 10 m terrain grid; shared nodes agree by construction. The lap is that field at its own XY, with sigma-12 m junction vertical curves held within 0.15 m of the field; the build then owns the runtime 14% two-metre cap using symmetric feasible envelopes (maximum field adjustment under 1.2 m on this route) and cuts the terrain after it, so `new Route()` changes nothing. Beside the lap the street bed is level across kerbs and footways and the ground keeps its real cross-slope; only the edge offset decays over 60 m. The research behind these choices is `.scratch/psychiko-realism/elevation/REPORT.md` (sources compared, Street View cross-check). The earlier EU-DEM `dem.json`/`route-dem.json`/`garden-route-dem.json` and `fetch_dem.py` are retired (git history).
- `raw/relations.json` (11 KB): the 56 multipolygon relations (buildings, parks, stadium/tracks, woods, land use) whose member ways the way/node snapshot already holds. The original extract never requested relations; these come from the official OSM API 0.6 map fallback the coverage audit fetched on 2026-10-19 (`fetch_osm.py --relations-from`, or `--relations` to query Overpass afresh). Courtyards stay open: a holed footprint becomes several ≤10-vertex blocks, within the owner's 40-triangle building cap.
- `corrections.mjs`: reviewed in-memory corrections applied by every builder and test; `raw/osm.json.gz` stays untouched. Plateia Eleftherias (park 491349809, ~33 m radius) is a circulating circle: rim 25053562 is `oneway=yes` CCW but untagged as a roundabout and closes through the `oneway=no` ends of 42110999 (272561334→272498881) and all of 717372563. Only those closing rim pieces join the ring (`junction=roundabout`, CCW); the Nikiforou Lytra and Diamantidou approaches stay two-way. The island is the ring's kerb inset; the park polygon itself overlaps the carriageway by up to ~1.5 m, so it remains planting.
- `raw/garden-route.json`: the first-version loop, kept so its streets stay in the drivable network.
- `raw/street-classes.json`: frozen reduction of the 2026-10-19 municipal Street View survey (1,034 accepted stations over 366 public ways inside relation 1403235), made once with `street-survey.mjs` from the survey's own JSON. It holds the 12 cross-section classes (kerb-to-kerb width including kerb parking, centre paint, footway width), each surveyed OSM way's class (mixed ways keep their station samples; the nearest station decides), observed kerb-parking counts per way and the 26 stations with readable boundary / vegetation / lamp / wire notes. Classes are visual size bands, not kerb measurements. No imagery is stored; Street View © Google was used for visual classification only.

The shared at-grade renderer omits the selected Kifisias tunnel ways (`25117418`, `113339568`, `113339581`) outside the lap. These underpasses need a grade-separated carriageway the at-grade kit cannot build, so they are not reconstructed; surface ribbons in their place would create false junctions.

Public ways are selected by municipal **clipped length** (stretches lying on the administrative edge count), as well as by vertex containment, the drawn loop, a 280 m lap corridor and the original course. This restores the boundary streets 25is Martiou `97722996` and Stefanou Delta `97723003`/`790219496`.

## Direction and roundabout guidance

Every `junction=roundabout` ring is circulated counter-clockwise by every driver: the racing line and rivals (forward only), civilian traffic (ring integrator, legal reverse plans within 60 m of the lap only), and the directed network ring parts. Free-roam civilians never run against a source one-way lap street: `route.oneway` lists the forward-only stretches (including the Areaki approaches 147731530 and 31269674); oncoming cars spawn only on two-way stretches and turn off at the junction before a one-way street. Each public ring (the ten municipal circles: nine tagged plus Eleftherias, the border loop and one Filothei loop) has painted CCW arrows, a dashed give-way line and a Greek P-1 give-way over P-40 mandatory-roundabout sign at each legal entry. Mini-roundabout node 351945852 gets a painted, mountable centre, CCW arrows and the same entry signs; civilians do not route on side streets, so its directed movements are guidance only. The gated Athens College drive 31270034 is left as it was. All guidance is one vertex-coloured mesh (one draw call).

### Municipal survey application (2026-10-19)

- **Cross-sections.** Every surveyed way takes its class from `raw/street-classes.json` instead of a name regex: A9 9 m and A8 8 m double-white avenues, R6 6 m, N5 5.5 m and N4 3.5 m unmarked streets, H6 hillside 6 m, P7 park edge 7 m, I7 Benaki school-wall 7 m (double white), F5 5 m frontage, D8 8 m and T10 10.5 m carriageways with dashed same-direction lane dividers. Widths are kerb to kerb including parking. Double solid centre lines only on two-way classed avenues. The lap's route names carry the class of each raced way (Benaki 7 m, Roidi 5.5 m, Prigkipisas Elenis 7 m, the Lytra approach 8 m). C7 rings and unsurveyed ways keep their earlier treatment. Footway widths follow the class (0.6-1.7 m).
- **Streetscape records** (`scenery.mjs`, stored compactly in `data.psychiko.scenery`, rendered by `src/maps/psychiko-streetscape.js`): boundary runs at the back of every footway with gaps at junctions and driveways (plaster + dark railing, hedge on a low stone base, metal fence, rough stone, plain plaster, metal gates between white posts) mixed by class from the survey's readable stations, the observed station's own boundary types within 35 m, and a tall rough-stone wall along school and college campuses, including the reviewed left boundary of Bizaniou (97723004) where the closed-way campus index lacks the campus relation. Species canopy: umbrella pines, slender cypresses, broadleaf, occasional palms, small/pollarded street trees and garden shrubs, placed as footway street trees, garden trees behind the walls, park margin rows and irregular canopy groups (woods pine-dominated), plus the mapped OSM trees and rows. Kerb parking rows on side-street asphalt use each way's observed occupancy (class share otherwise), both sides only where at least 3.6 m stays usable; on the lap's A9/A8 sections cars sit astride the kerb outside the raced width, outside the start grid lanes, with gaps at the gantry, rings, mouths, crossings and driveways. Green gooseneck lamps with cream bells stagger along the avenues; utility poles with side-arm lamps carry overhead wire spans on other streets. Zebra crossings at OSM marked crossings inside the municipality and on the A square, B square and Pindou approach arms.
- **Roofs.** Footprints with the pitched flag (OSM `roof:shape` overrides it; gabled stays gabled) get a tiled hipped roof over their minimum-area rectangle when the footprint is close to rectangular; other footprints keep flat roofs and the residential height scale. Rooftop plant is not placed on pitched roofs.
- **Footways.** Pink tiles on Diamantidou and around the squares, pale slabs elsewhere.
- **Square gardens and avenues:** the inset island polygons carry irregular pine/broadleaf groups, low shrubs and interrupted perimeter hedges, keeping open centre/path corridors. A/B squares, Kamara, Areaki and Eleftherias have distinct crown counts and garden layouts, inferred from survey notes rather than exact trunk positions. Goosenecks stand on the outer kerb; generic lap lamps are omitted on ring arcs. A9/A8 footway rows use roughly 7.5 m spacing and mature broadleaf crowns. Solid avenue paint remains continuous with modest wear; D8/T10 retain dashed lane dividers. Full-size ring arrows are 4.2 m long and retain CCW tangents; mini-roundabout arrows are unchanged.
- **Not done here:** satellite canopy positions from Google imagery were deliberately not used; per-station lamp types beyond the avenue/side-street split, kerb heights and individual walls remain class estimates.

## Rebuild offline

```sh
node tools/maps/psychiko/plan-faros.mjs     # only after reviewing/changing the leg list
node tools/maps/psychiko/route.mjs          # raw/drawn-course.json -> raw/route.json (+ raw/route-points.json)
node tools/maps/psychiko/street-survey.mjs <survey-dir>  # only when re-reducing the Street View survey -> raw/street-classes.json
node tools/maps/psychiko/build.mjs          # data/maps/psychiko/{map.json,terrain.bin}, incl. shared speed signs
node tools/build-detail-records.mjs         # adds the OSM vegetation / facade records to every map bundle
node tools/maps/psychiko/validate.mjs
node tools/maps/psychiko/preview.mjs        # assets/maps/psychiko.svg
node --test tools/psychiko-world.test.mjs
ATTICA_PYTHON=<venv with numpy pillow shapely scipy> node tools/build-attica-map.mjs   # selector route, pin and detail art
```

No live fetch is needed except for refreshing sources deliberately: `python3 tools/maps/psychiko/fetch_osm.py` (and `--relations`), `uv run --no-project --with rasterio python3 tools/maps/psychiko/fetch_copernicus.py`, then review source adjacency, access and height changes before rebuilding. `route.mjs && build.mjs && build-detail-records.mjs` reproduce `map.json` and `terrain.bin` byte-for-byte.

`tools/psychiko-world.test.mjs` covers: route/selector/server consistency; both events tracking the whole route; continuous terrain/contact and the carved street bed; all public municipal roads selected; every closure (one per outgoing public junction half-edge, none on the raced edges, solid at 90 km/h in races, absent in free roam); the Faros Circuit following public OSM streets only (within 4.5 m of a street centreline everywhere), driving every street in its legal one-way direction, starting at the Faros surface light, smooth corners, and being a new leaderboard/personal-best board. `VERIFICATION.md` records the 2026-10-18 end-to-end drive and screenshots.

### Rendering changes for the expanded frontage

Psychiko keeps the same 2.5 m terrain contact grid. Only the immediate road/shoulder band is refined (12 m beyond the street width, instead of an unused 32 m band). The terrain scan still checks every uploaded triangle, all streets and six-metre shoulders. Network road chords are within 2 cm of the shared height field; painted lanes remain shader work. Race barriers are merged by 200 m cell/material, with one logical root and physical collider per closed exit. Static signals/medians reuse the existing near-prop cells. Nearby folded carriageway edges use a stricter shared-height constraint so sharp junctions do not leave a kerb-sized terrain step. These adjustments apply only to Psychiko's street-circuit/garden policy.

Reference-only Google imagery for seven requested stations was fetched with the existing municipal survey transport (no API key). The side-by-sides label **Street View © Google**, record panorama positions/headings, and never feed imagery or derived textures into the game or deployment. Faros/Elikonos signal locations and arterial lane counts come from OSM; dressing remains an approximation.
