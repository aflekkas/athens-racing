# Psychiko sources and reproduction

Revision: 2026-10-19 (Garden Circuit v2, Copernicus hills, one-way roundabouts). Fictional closed-road event on real public streets. The owner-requested redesign starts and finishes on the Diamantidou radial south of the Venizelou roundabout (OSM node 272499018 is the Parnithos/Agiou Dimitriou junction the lap passes just after the line) and loops the Palaio Psychiko fan on public OSM streets only, in their legal one-way direction. Psychiko has **no reverse direction** (solo, event resolution and multiplayer): its one-way streets and four raced rings (Kamara, Areaki, Eleftherias rim, Venizelou) are legal only forwards. The lap is **3,753.4 m**; `psychiko-race` is two laps with two rivals. `free:psychiko` is the existing shared untimed free-roam event, with traffic/street life and no roadworks. `psychiko-tt` stays disabled. Earlier loops (the 5,960.9 m / 6,377.3 m drawn loop and the 4,285.7 m garden loop) are superseded; their times are a separate board.

## Frozen inputs

- `raw/osm.json.gz`: original 2026-10-14 primary [Overpass](https://overpass-api.de/api/interpreter) response for south 37.992 / west 23.758 / north 38.027 / east 23.799. Nodes, ways and source IDs are retained. © [OpenStreetMap contributors](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright), ODbL 1.0.
- `raw/boundaries.json`: Overpass response with municipal relation [1403235](https://www.openstreetmap.org/relation/1403235) for Palaio Psychiko. The public motor-road network inside the municipality, the area enclosed by the lap and ways near the lap are selected. Public-road selection excludes private/no motor access, private driveways and non-motor paths.
- `raw/drawn-course.json`: the exact OSM node chain and one source way per edge of the lap, plus `startOffset` (metres along the loop from its first node; negative counts back), which puts the timing line on the Diamantidou radial. It was produced by searching the frozen street graph for closed, legal-direction loops through that radial (smooth, few turns, roundabouts, 3.5-6 km) and choosing the best by eye against the OSM map; `route.mjs` verifies every edge against the snapshot (adjacency, public access, paved surface). `raw/route.json` is its deterministic result: junction corners are filleted (10 m radius, bounded by the street so the line stays within about 4 m of the OSM centreline), then lightly smoothed and resampled at about 2 m.
- `raw/copernicus.json.gz` (54 KB): [Copernicus DEM GLO-30 Public, 2021 release](https://copernicus-dem-30m.s3.amazonaws.com/readme.html), range-read from the public AWS COGs over the whole terrain bbox plus 0.002° (`fetch_copernicus.py`, 2026-10-19; windows of tiles N37/N38 E023, integer centimetres, EGM2008). © DLR e.V. 2010-2014 and © Airbus Defence and Space GmbH 2014-2018 provided under COPERNICUS by the European Union and ESA. It is a surface model (~30 m), not a surveyed bare-earth DTM. `elevation.mjs` mosaics the windows, applies one **sigma-20 m spatial Gaussian** and fits each complete roundabout ring with one least-squares plane along its carriageway (blended back over 20 m). That single field gives the lap, every side road, junction, ring and the 10 m terrain grid; shared nodes agree by construction. The lap is that field at its own XY, with sigma-12 m junction vertical curves held within 0.15 m of the field; the build then owns the runtime 14% two-metre cap (about 0.5 m on the Diamantidou descent) and cuts the terrain after it, so `new Route()` changes nothing. Beside the lap the street bed is level across kerbs and footways and the ground keeps its real cross-slope; only the edge offset decays over 35 m. The research behind these choices is `.scratch/psychiko-realism/elevation/REPORT.md` (sources compared, Street View cross-check). The earlier EU-DEM `dem.json`/`route-dem.json`/`garden-route-dem.json` and `fetch_dem.py` are retired (git history).
- `raw/relations.json` (11 KB): the 56 multipolygon relations (buildings, parks, stadium/tracks, woods, land use) whose member ways the way/node snapshot already holds. The original extract never requested relations; these come from the official OSM API 0.6 map fallback the coverage audit fetched on 2026-10-19 (`fetch_osm.py --relations-from`, or `--relations` to query Overpass afresh). Courtyards stay open: a holed footprint becomes several ≤10-vertex blocks, within the owner's 40-triangle building cap.
- `corrections.mjs`: reviewed in-memory corrections applied by every builder and test; `raw/osm.json.gz` stays untouched. Plateia Eleftherias (park 491349809, ~33 m radius) is a circulating circle: rim 25053562 is `oneway=yes` CCW but untagged as a roundabout and closes through the `oneway=no` ends of 42110999 (272561334→272498881) and all of 717372563. Only those closing rim pieces join the ring (`junction=roundabout`, CCW); the Nikiforou Lytra and Diamantidou approaches stay two-way. The island is the ring's kerb inset; the park polygon itself overlaps the carriageway by up to ~1.5 m, so it remains planting.
- `raw/garden-route.json`: the first-version loop, kept so its streets stay in the drivable network.

The shared at-grade renderer omits the selected Kifisias tunnel ways (`25117418`, `113339568`, `113339581`) outside the lap. These underpasses need a grade-separated carriageway the at-grade kit cannot build, so they are not reconstructed; surface ribbons in their place would create false junctions.

Public ways are selected by municipal **clipped length** (stretches lying on the administrative edge count), as well as by vertex containment, the drawn loop, a 280 m lap corridor and the original course. This restores the boundary streets 25is Martiou `97722996` and Stefanou Delta `97723003`/`790219496`.

## Direction and roundabout guidance

Every `junction=roundabout` ring is circulated counter-clockwise by every driver: the racing line and rivals (forward only), civilian traffic (ring integrator, legal reverse plans within 60 m of the lap only), and the directed network ring parts. Free-roam civilians never run against a source one-way lap street: `route.oneway` lists the forward-only stretches (including the Areaki approaches 147731530 and 31269674); oncoming cars spawn only on two-way stretches and turn off at the junction before a one-way street. Each public ring (the ten municipal circles: nine tagged plus Eleftherias, the border loop and one Filothei loop) has painted CCW arrows, a dashed give-way line and a Greek P-1 give-way over P-40 mandatory-roundabout sign at each legal entry. Mini-roundabout node 351945852 gets a painted, mountable centre, CCW arrows and the same entry signs; civilians do not route on side streets, so its directed movements are guidance only. The gated Athens College drive 31270034 is left as it was. All guidance is one vertex-coloured mesh (one draw call).

## Street appearance

The first-version street references were personally inspected before modelling, with attribution retained in ignored `.scratch/psychiko/` PNGs:

- [Diamantidou 42, south-facing](https://www.google.com/maps/@?api=1&map_action=pano&pano=9Q4JR2V_ywleu6_RNyrl8Q&heading=180&pitch=0&fov=90), September 2024, 38.0126551 / 23.7723670: pine/broadleaf canopy, stone/plaster walls, metal fencing, villa setbacks, tiled footways and double centre lines.
- [Agiou Dimitriou 50 / Kallari](https://www.google.com/maps/@?api=1&map_action=pano&pano=OM1Pkpuc2Z4EJ7VKZpgIow&heading=210&pitch=0&fov=90), April 2026, 38.0118376 / 23.7787348: broad junction, mature pines, garden hedges, narrow footways and street signs.
- Context: [Czech Embassy directions](https://mzv.gov.cz/athens/en/kontakty.html), [BLP Diamantidou villa](https://blp.gr/projects/psychico-house-diamantidou-street/).

This implementation reuses the shared Greek street, traffic, people, nature and drivable-network systems. No source imagery is shipped as textures. Building footprints are OSM-derived; missing levels, road widths, facades, planting, walls and garden boundaries are estimates. A separate Street View research pass in `.scratch/psychiko-streetview/` is intentionally untouched and has not yet been applied to this revision's scenery.

## Rebuild offline

```sh
node tools/maps/psychiko/route.mjs          # raw/drawn-course.json -> raw/route.json (+ raw/route-points.json)
node tools/maps/psychiko/build.mjs          # data/maps/psychiko/{map.json,terrain.bin}, incl. shared speed signs
node tools/build-detail-records.mjs         # adds the OSM vegetation / facade records to every map bundle
node tools/maps/psychiko/validate.mjs
node tools/maps/psychiko/preview.mjs        # assets/maps/psychiko.svg
node --test tools/psychiko-world.test.mjs
ATTICA_PYTHON=<venv with numpy pillow shapely scipy> node tools/build-attica-map.mjs   # selector route, pin and detail art
```

No live fetch is needed except for refreshing sources deliberately: `python3 tools/maps/psychiko/fetch_osm.py` (and `--relations`), `uv run --no-project --with rasterio python3 tools/maps/psychiko/fetch_copernicus.py`, then review source adjacency, access and height changes before rebuilding. `route.mjs && build.mjs && build-detail-records.mjs` reproduce `map.json` and `terrain.bin` byte-for-byte.

## Verification evidence

`tools/psychiko-world.test.mjs` covers: route/selector/server consistency; both events tracking the whole route; continuous terrain/contact and the carved street bed; all public municipal roads selected; every closure (one per outgoing public junction half-edge, none on the raced edges, solid at 90 km/h in races, absent in free roam); the Garden Circuit following public OSM streets only (within 4.5 m of a street centreline everywhere), driving every street in its legal one-way direction, starting on the Diamantidou radial south of the roundabout, smooth corners, and being a new leaderboard/personal-best board. `VERIFICATION.md` records the 2026-10-18 end-to-end drive and screenshots.
