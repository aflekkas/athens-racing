# Additional selectable Greek-road cars

All geometry is original procedural work in `src/player-road-cars.js` and dedicated car modules; photographs are reference-only, stored with their URLs in `.scratch/models-more/refs/sources.json`. No reference photograph is used as a game texture. Power is in kW in the catalogue (PS/CV in the display names); torque is Nm, dimensions metres, mass kg. Full torque curves, static axle loads, tyre friction, ride/damping frequencies and shift timing are simulation estimates unless stated otherwise.

## MINI John Cooper Works F56 (`mini_jcw`)

2015 European six-speed manual hatchback, **170 kW / 231 PS, 320 Nm**, 1205 kg DIN, FWD, 246 km/h. Dimensions 3.874 × 1.727 × 1.414 m, wheelbase 2.495 m, 205/45 R17 tyres. BMW's [March 2015 specification sheet](https://www.press.bmwgroup.com/global/article/attachment/T0210262EN/303092) supplies the dimensions, mass, output, 3.824 final drive, all six manual ratios, tyres and 0.71 m² CdA. [Launch press kit](https://www.press.bmwgroup.com/global/article/detail/T0197651EN/the-new-mini-john-cooper-works?language=en) and [interior press image](https://www.press.bmwgroup.com/global/photo/detail/P90182009/mini-john-cooper-works-05/2015?language=en) support the closed roof, round light modules, bigger brake intakes, centre exhaust, spoiler, round centre console and toggles. [F56 JCW reference photograph](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Mini_F56_JCW_IMG_0927.jpg).

The model uses the existing four-corner steering/spin/suspension hierarchy, player cabin and driver integration, seated live mirror cameras, independent lamp materials, Greek plates, per-instance paint, deformable/detachable damage parts and near/medium/far LODs. `playerDetail:true` opts additional catalogue vehicles into this pipeline. Both multiplayer backends derive their accepted IDs from CARS: the server reads the source, the Worker consumes `tools/prepare-deploy.mjs`'s generated `.deploy/catalogue.json`.

## Lamborghini Huracán EVO AWD (`huracan`)

2019 EVO AWD coupe, **470 kW / 640 CV at 8000 rpm, 600 Nm at 6500**, seven-speed LDF, rated >325 km/h and 2.9 s to 100. Dimensions 4.520 × 1.933 × 1.165 m, wheelbase 2.620 m. [Lamborghini model specification and design description](https://www.lamborghini.com/en-en/history/huracan-evo) supports the power, performance, AWD, seven-speed DCT, Y-shaped intakes and high-mounted exhausts. Manufacturer dry mass is 1422 kg; **1542 kg in the game is an estimated road-ready mass**, not a factory kerb-weight claim. The gearbox uses estimated effective overall ratios for the Huracán family (split final-drive layout), not independently verified EVO homologation ratios. The 70% rear torque share is a simplified default; there is no full LDVI or rear-steering simulation. Tyres: 245/30 ZR20 front, 305/30 ZR20 rear. Static 43:57 distribution and 0.68 m² CdA are model estimates.

Dedicated low two-seat cabin, portrait console screen, central digital tachometer, side intakes, deck louvres, rear diffuser and raised exhausts distinguish it from the conventional hatchback meshes. The acceleration regression now uses a tolerance around a declared manufacturer 0–100 figure when supplied, retaining the old 3–14 s envelope for existing entries; this accommodates both a 2.9-second supercar and genuinely slow economy cars.

## SEAT Ibiza 6K 1.4 (`ibiza`)

Early five-door 1.4i CLX, **44 kW / 60 PS, 107 Nm**, FWD, five-speed manual, 935 kg representative road mass, 157 km/h. The [1994 SEAT Netherlands brochure, p. 8](https://automobilisto.de/nl/archive/seat/ibiza/1994-nl.pdf) supplies 3.813 × 1.640 × 1.414 m dimensions, 2.440 m wheelbase, 157 km/h and 15.7 s to 100 for the 1.4i. Gear ratios, CdA and the full torque curve are approximations. The [SEAT heritage archive](https://www.seatcocheshistoricos.es/SEATHistoricos/en/1990/ibizacopa.php) documents the 6K generation.

Split grille, upright rectangular lamps, black bumper strips, steel wheels, rear drums, period radio, faded paint finishes and small sill rust patches. This is the **only catalogue entry with `nitrous:true`**; the bottle and valve sit behind the seats. Worn colours reduce clearcoat and increase paint roughness. Standard safety aids are gameplay options, not claims that the original car had ESC.

## Toyota Yaris XP10 1.0 (`yaris`)

1999–2002 five-door **50 kW / 68 PS, 90 Nm**, 850 kg representative base mass, FWD, five-speed manual, 155 km/h. Dimensions 3.615 × 1.660 × 1.500 m, wheelbase 2.370 m. Primary references: [Toyota first-generation press archive](https://media.toyota.co.uk/vehicles/yaris-archive-1999/), [1999 launch](https://media.toyota.co.uk/the-new-toyota-yaris/) and [complete first-generation press pack](https://media.toyota.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/sites/5/pdf/Gen1-Yaris-archive-press-pack.pdf). ## BMW E36 325i sedan (`e36`)

Representative 1993 M50TU five-speed sedan, **141 kW / 192 PS, 245 Nm**, 1330 kg, **RWD**, 233 km/h. Dimensions 4.433 × 1.698 × 1.393 m, wheelbase 2.700 m. [BMW Classic's 325i entry](https://www.bmwgroup-classic.com/de/modelle/bmw-klassiker/product-description-page.ad-464-7.bmw-325i-e36.html) confirms the 2494 cc six, output at 5900 rpm and 233 km/h. The road mass, dimensions and gearbox represent common European manual specifications; tyre friction, 25% limited-slip preload, CdA and suspension/aids are simulation choices. The limited-slip differential is an optional/modified-car setup, not standard-equipment attribution.

Long bonnet, short front overhang, four-door three-box body, twin kidneys, four round lamp modules behind rectangular covers, amber corners and period radio. It fills the cheap rear-drive slot and sustains power-oversteer with TC/ESC disabled.

## Catalogue calibration and verification

The 155/80 R13 tyres on the Ibiza, Panda and Yaris use an estimated 170 N/mm vertical stiffness instead of the shared 240 N/mm default. This retains their soft 1.55 Hz suspension and reduces smooth-road load swing to 8.5%, 9.2% and 9.5% respectively. The Huracán road setup uses an estimated 1.9 Hz ride frequency and 0.80 default traction-control strength: the measured roll gradient is 1.59 deg/g against the effective setup's 1.87, and the existing sand reverse-out scenarios pass. These are simulation calibration values, not claimed factory measurements. Power, mass, gearing and the test thresholds are unchanged by this calibration.

The cockpit seats and Huracán mirror stalk positions were checked using the actual driver-eye rays. All six cars pass the natural FOV, open header/horizon and three-mirror visibility tests. MINI bonnet shoulders retain the headlamp shape while its lower centre clears the forward road view. Bumper trim is split at the existing damage boundary so its upper strip stays attached; subdivided number plates follow the rounded fascia without being buried in it.

Reference comparisons and five views per car are in `.scratch/models-more/{id}-comparison.jpg` and `{id}-{front,rear,side,interior,chase}.png`. The chase captures use the running Megara game and its normal snapped chase camera. References with source URLs are in `refs/sources.json`; rejected wrong-generation photographs are marked unused.

Final validation logs in that directory: `npm-test-final.log` (105 passing), `tools-tests-final.log` (118 passing, run with the physics-test loader), `suspension-final.log` (215 passing), `sand-final.log` (all scenarios), `regress-final.log` (108 passing), `drift-all.log` (all 20 cars), and `driver-feel[-power]-final.log`. `final-qa.json` records 200 vehicle checks, 18 new-player budget checks, 144 mirror attachment checks, 210 cockpit optical checks, 140 garage-paint checks, 228 new-player damage/detail checks and 123 traffic checks for the six additions plus Golf. `traction-final.json` compares identical Huracán setups: AWD has zero driven-wheel overspeed in the corner exit, versus measurable spin with FWD/RWD; RWD is faster in that particular exit, so this is a traction comparison rather than a lap-time claim.

Car commits: MINI `0508dd4`, Huracán `a3f3089`, Ibiza `838a2a2`, Panda `91fcee0`, Yaris `b62a409`, E36 `fdd0278`. The MINI's initial catalogue/common-pipeline integration was captured by concurrent shared-index commit `2aa7581`; that history was preserved. Distinct V10/straight-six synthesis is `7305d87`; suspension/sand calibration is `2ccdae2`. No push or deployment was performed.



## Ferrari 348 tb (`f348`)

1989–1993 fixed-roof berlinetta, **3405 cc F119 90° flat-plane V8**, longitudinal behind the seats, transverse five-speed manual and RWD. [Ferrari's model archive](https://www.ferrari.com/en-EN/auto/348-tb) and [history entry](https://www.ferrari.com/en-TD/history/garage/1989/348-tb) identify the 300 CV tb, distinct from the later 320 CV GTB. [Technical reference](https://www.ultimatespecs.com/car-specs/Ferrari/1960/Ferrari-348-TB.html): **221 kW at 7200 rpm, 324 Nm at 4200 rpm, 275 km/h, 5.6 s to 100**, 4.230 × 1.894 × 1.170 m and 2.450 m wheelbase. Use **1500 kg representative road-ready kerb mass**, also listed by [the 348 overview](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ferrari_348), instead of treating the published **1393 kg dry** figure as a fuelled car. Mass conventions and equipment vary.

Five-speed ratios 3.214 / 2.105 / 1.458 / 1.094 / .861 and 3.563 final follow the rounded values in [this period-specification compilation](https://www.supercars.net/blog/1990-ferrari-348-tb/); they are representative rather than independently homologation-verified for every market. Tyres are 215/50 ZR17 front and 255/45 ZR17 rear (the technical reference above incorrectly lists a 50-profile rear); 0.331 m is a common effective rolling radius. Track 1.540 m averages the reported 1.502/1.578 m axles because this physics model has one track parameter. The 7500 rpm limiter, 1000 rpm idle, full torque curve between output anchors, 41:59 static distribution, .62 m² drag area, inertia, suspension, differential, grip and aids are simulation calibration choices. TC/ESC remain player options, rather than claims about period factory equipment. Lower yaw inertia and a peakier tyre curve provide sharp turn-in and lively breakaway with aids disabled. Stats sit below the modern Carrera/Huracán for acceleration and grip, above the E36/GTV for performance.

`v8flat` is a self-contained synthesized eight-cylinder profile with nearly even pulse strength, bright upper harmonics and no turbo lag; it does not modify or depend on a cross-plane `v8` profile. `engineZone:'rear'` uses the game's rear mechanical-damage routing for the mid-mounted engine. The only physics-core change is an additive engine-map entry: otherwise an unknown `v8flat` would inherit turbo-four lag and inertia.

Original procedural geometry in `src/player-f348.js`: crowned wedge nose, flush closed pop-up lids and shut lines, horizontal painted strakes over recessed intakes, black rear grille over rectangular tail lenses, flying buttresses around a recessed rear window, engine-deck louvres, five-spoke alloys and four exhaust tips. Two-seat tan cabin, round analogue instruments, period radio and exposed aluminium dogleg gate; Auto/Manual remains the player's setting. Tiny badge is an original drawn rearing-horse silhouette. Rosso Corsa, Giallo Modena, Nero, Argento Nürburgring, Blu Tour de France and Bianco are screen approximations requested for this car; modern shade names are not a claim that every name was offered during 1989–1993. No reference photographs become textures.

Initial model validation: full `TMPDIR=/private/tmp npm test` passes (266 passed, one skipped). The explicit TMPDIR avoids the existing macOS `/var` versus `/private/var` symlink mismatch in the bundle fixture; production bundling code is unchanged. The stored automatic-trajectory test already failed with the original physics on this ARM64 Mac (also reproduced on Node 22); its maximum relative deviation was 2.23e-14. Only that stored float comparison now allows 1e-12 relative tolerance; discrete gears and the live default/explicit-Auto comparison remain exact, and the golden fixture is unchanged. A separate before/after run of all 20 pre-existing cars for 900 frames each is bit-identical. Physics regression: 112 passed; suspension: 225 passed. The 348 bench measures 5.83 s to 100, 270.1 km/h after the regression's top-speed run and 35.2 m from 100–0. An aggressive four-second 60 km/h corner reaches 8.7° maximum sideslip with aids and spins with them off. Browser model checks: 124 passed, no console errors. Offline engine audio tests pass, including eight-cylinder `v8flat` output (peak .354, RMS .148), alongside all existing procedural and recorded engines; the fixture intentionally requests one missing WAV to verify fallback handling.

Initial headless Chromium verification used `PORT=8104 npm start`: the actual Sports catalogue lists the car, its garage shows all six finishes and 5.6 s, and keyboard throttle in Megara free roam moved it about 16 m from rest to 39.4 km/h. No game console/page errors. Inspected `/tmp/cars-f348/car-select.png`, `garage.png`, `chase.png`, plus `front.png`, `rear.png`, `side.png` and `interior.png`; scripts and JSON/log evidence are alongside them. ### Reference refinement pass

Compared three photos of a **1991 348 tb**, Barrett-Jackson Scottsdale 2017 lot 989.2: [front three-quarter](https://barrettjacksoncdn.azureedge.net/staging/carlist/items/Fullsize/Cars/203495/203495_Front_3-4_Web.jpg), [side profile](https://barrettjacksoncdn.azureedge.net/staging/carlist/items/Fullsize/Cars/203495/203495_Side_Profile_Web.jpg), [rear three-quarter](https://barrettjacksoncdn.azureedge.net/staging/carlist/items/Fullsize/Cars/203495/203495_Rear_3-4_Web.jpg). Downloads and source URLs are under `/tmp/realism-f348/refs/`; none are game textures or committed files. This particular example has wing shields, which are not added to the model.

- Flat section transitions and square end caps → smoothly interpolated sections, crowned bonnet/wing tops, rolled deck edges and rounded bumper corners. The lenses, plates, lower trim and exhaust effect anchors follow the same end mapping. Solid-colour paint has reduced metallic content and a glossier base finish; the shared per-instance paint/clearcoat pipeline remains active.
- Permanent raised headlamp boxes → flush closed painted covers with sampled shut lines. These are **static closed covers**, including with headlights on; no opening animation or raised nighttime assembly is implemented. The bumper driving lamps retain the shared light material.
- Square front and protruding black rail → a rounded painted bumper, slim central grille, inset rectangular clear/amber lamp units, wrapping lower black lip and small centred silver horse. Bonnet panel seams and the yellow nose badge follow the curved skin.
- Oversized rectangular intake ladder → a lower opening, five tapered painted blades, increasing intake depth toward the rear and a fuller smooth door panel above. Black sill/quarter trim wraps into both lower valances. Rounded mirror shells and gently surfaced flying-buttress panels retain the original mount/camera hooks.
- Thick engine-deck bars and flat tail trim → finer slots seated on the engine cover, sampled tail grille ribs with depth over independently visible rectangular red/amber lenses, a small silver horse, black lower rear panel and four retained exhaust tips.
- Thin straight wheel bars → broad, bevelled, tapering five-point cast spokes. Existing 17-inch rims, 215/255 mm tyre widths, rolling radius, wheel pivots, suspension and all handling values remain unchanged.

Three inspected intermediate rounds are saved as `round1-*`, `round2-*`, `round3-*` under `/tmp/realism-f348/`; final matching studio captures are `before-{front,side,rear,top,straight,interior}.png` and `after-{front,side,rear,top,straight,interior}.png`. `comparison.html` shows reference/before/after views. The paint is still affected strongly by the studio's bright environment; the cabin fittings, mirror mounting shape and fine panel/strake end radii remain procedural approximations.

## Porsche 928 GTS (`p928`)

1992–1995 five-speed manual transaxle GT: **5397 cc 32-valve front V8, 257 kW / 350 PS at 5700 rpm, 500 Nm at 4250 rpm**, 6600 rpm fuel cut, 1620 kg, RWD with limited slip. Engine anchors and firing order come from the [Porsche owner's manual, p. 104](https://www.manualslib.de/manual/248463/Porsche-928-Gts.html?page=104); [p. 105](https://www.manualslib.de/manual/248463/Porsche-928-Gts.html?page=105) supplies 3.775 / 2.519 / 1.795 / 1.358 / 1.000 ratios and 2.727 final drive. [Porsche Classic](https://www.porsche.com/spain/accessoriesandservice/classic/models/928/928-gt-gts/) documents the 5.4-litre GTS, broad rear quarters and rear light strip. Reference targets are 275 km/h and 5.7 s to 100; dimensions from the brief are 4.520 × 1.890 × 1.282 m, wheelbase 2.500 m. [Porsche's transaxle history](https://newsroom.porsche.com/christophorus/en/2017/384/928-top-model-1977-engine-transaxle-geneva-motor-show-long-distance-world-record-14487.html) describes the front engine/rear gearbox arrangement.

The requested **225/40 ZR18 front / 265/35 ZR18 rear Cup-style upgrade** is fitted (0.2286 m rim radius). It is not an attribution of standard factory GTS tyre equipment: the original manual lists 225/45 ZR17 and 255/40 ZR17. Near-even static axle loading, 40% LSD strength, torque interpolation away from the sourced peaks, drag area, suspension, grip and optional TC/ESC strengths are simulation estimates. Auto/Manual remains the player's choice. The dedicated `v8` sound uses synthesized cross-plane firing pulses and low burble, independent of flat-plane V8 profiles or downloaded recordings.

Original geometry lives in `src/player-p928.js`: low sloping bonnet, circular exposed lenses that flip forward when the headlamps switch on, rounded tapered ends, wide rear haunches, full-width dark red reflector panel, large glass hatch, body-colour rear bridge spoiler, Cup spokes and seven period paint approximations. It uses the common cabin, steering/suspension hierarchy, mirror seating, lighting, damage and distance LOD pipeline. `finishModel` retains the moving lamp meshes before compaction/damage registration; `updateHeadlamps` animates only the opted-in car. Distant simplified LOD lamps remain static.

Validation evidence is in `/tmp/cars-p928/`: the full npm suite (canonical `TMPDIR=/private/tmp` avoids a macOS temp-path symlink issue), 111 physics regressions, the 928 handling/drift bench, 38 player-model checks, browser WebAudio synthesis/lifecycle tests and headless garage/chase captures. Flat-road simulation measures 5.20 s to 100, 271.2 km/h after 60 s and 33.6 m braking from 100; these are simulation results, distinct from reference specifications. Aided power rotation stays below 9°, and aids-off drift/transition/slalom checks pass. The frozen Auto trajectory test now allows a 1e-9 absolute numerical tolerance for saved values across CPU/math implementations; its live default-versus-explicit-Auto comparison remains bit-exact.

Realism refinement (2026-10-15): photographs of a silver 1993 GTS in the [BaT gallery](https://bringatrailer.com/listing/1993-porsche-928-gts-26/) anchor the side profile, front three-quarter, rear three-quarter and closed-lamp/wing detail. Local copies and source URLs are in `/tmp/realism-p928/refs/` (uncommitted). Compared with those photos, the previous shell had a short rearward greenhouse, straight roof/hatch edges, a pointed low nose, clear side glass, and small rectangular rear lenses. The refinement moves the cowl/header forward, gives the roof and hatch a shared continuous arc, tapers the quarter window above the rear axle, narrows the dark B-pillar, aligns the door shut/handle, and adds an independent dielectric side/hatch tint plus headliner. The nose is fuller, wing tops are crowned, closed pop-up lenses conform to the wings, bumper lamps/intake follow the rounded cap, and the broader rear light/reflector band follows a fuller rounded tail. Exterior dimensions, wheelbase, the existing staggered 18-inch wheel upgrade and all handling values are retained. The visual seat/eye position follows the revised greenhouse and passes cockpit visibility checks.
