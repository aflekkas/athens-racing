# Huracán EVO reference pass (2026-10-14)

The `huracan` physics specification in `src/cars.js` is unchanged. The body now has a dedicated folded shell, a continuous crowned roof arc, raked windscreen, broad rear buttresses, recessed six-sided sill intakes, Y LED optics, a hex mesh rear grille, slim rear lamps and 20-inch forked Aesir-style wheels. The requested hexagonal exhaust apertures are a deliberate styling deviation: production EVO press photos show round central outlets.

Reference images and URL/credit manifest: `.scratch/models-lambo/refs/urls.json`. Official manufacturer page: https://www.lamborghini.com/en-en/history/huracan-evo. Official exterior, rear, cockpit and design images are retained alongside the 2019 manufacturer press side photograph mirrored at https://www.newcarcars.com/nm1869/2019_lamborghini_huracan_evo/. Reference files are for local comparison, not shipped game textures.

Visual axle centres are +0.13 m relative to the body centre; wheelbase and tyre radius are unchanged. The shell's sill is 0.13 m above the wheel-ground plane and its arch clearance is 27 mm. The roof crown is 1.165 m. Windshield and rear-window edges share their roof boundary exactly. Cabin fittings, damage boundaries, four suspension hooks, LOD and three mirror anchors are retained.

Projection comparison and in-game evidence are in `.scratch/models-lambo/`. The overlay aligns wheel centres with one uniform scale and translation, without stretching the render independently in X/Y. Photo perspective and body taper are retained as limits on silhouette measurements; these are projected-image checks, not surveyed physical dimensions.

Final silhouette review: the engine-cover edge now descends from 0.915 m at the rear glazing to a 0.78 m tail, finished by an 18 mm ducktail lip. A geometric shoulder rises over the rear arch, with 45 mm of upper-flank inset and a hollow lower door. The rear arch clearance drops from 27 to 16 mm and its lip is more pronounced; the intake stays recessed. Wheel/physics values and LOD selection/budgets are unchanged. Final registered 48% overlay and rear-quarter evidence: `.scratch/models-lambo/final-silhouette/`.


Wedge/waist revision after the 443663c review: the windscreen, crown, rear glass and cover now share one continuous profile; the visual crown is 1.137 m before its thin rails. The nose edge falls to 0.35 m and its cowl joins the curved windscreen. The shoulder has a lower rearward-rising crease, a hollow lower door and up to 145 mm of upper-side tuck between the arches. Window sills curve down into the door and terminate at a rear point. The tail narrows in plan and below its shoulder, closes against the curved engine cover, and carries wrapped dark lamp pockets, thin LED signatures, a lower mesh band and a thin curved lip. Carpet, door cards and undertray are inset to stay inside the sculpted skin. Signal lamps follow the new corner curvature. Physics, wheel dimensions, suspension hooks, damage groups and LOD selection rules remain unchanged.

Evidence: `.scratch/models-lambo/wedge-v4/photo-side-by-side.jpg`, `photo-overlay.png` (48%), `rear-quarter-sheet.jpg`, `rear-overlay.png`, and `silhouette-metrics.json`. Both sheets use uniform wheelbase registration, never independent axis stretching. Side samples have maximum vertical errors of 3 px at the roof, 2 px at the nose, and 7.1 px at the window sill (1.2%, 0.8%, 2.8% of the photograph's 255 px vehicle height; manual contour uncertainty approximately ±3 px). The rear press view is separately fitted from wheel centres and tyre radii; it is an oblique visual comparison, not a claim of equivalent contour accuracy. The deliberately hexagonal exhausts differ from the round production EVO outlets. Source credits remain in `refs/urls.json`.
