Current Riviera is Vouliagmeni–Lomvarda, not Alimos. Poseidonos/Alimou and historic Kalamaki are outside the playable corridor. Do not transplant them.

Ermou is **on the existing map**, immediately before the normal race start. Public OSM raw road data `data/raw/roads.json` (base timestamp 2026-10-13T14:35:13Z) has shared junction nodes 7542589901 (37.8127172, 23.7818855) and 277033598 (37.81275, 23.7820014), joining Ermou ways to Poseidonos ways 459160388/459160393/459160389/657717627. The representative junction centre (37.81273, 23.78194) projects to s=220m, 0.58m from `data/map.json` centreline; race start is s=241m. These coordinates locate the **junction**, not either camera post. The regression fixture uses this researched junction explicitly as a fixture and makes no real placement claim.
