# Sound realism and orientation audit (2026-10-14)

External traffic and rival StereoPanners already had the correct horizontal sign. Defects were fixed per-wheel player panning (an uneven loaded/sliding contact could pull sound to one ear), sea ambience following vehicle yaw instead of head look, and missing AudioListener camera position/forward/up updates. `audio-listener.js` now supplies both the Web Audio listener and stereo projection from the camera world matrix, including roll and mouse look. A right-hand source has positive pan in chase and cockpit. Own engine and contact-patch buses explicitly downmix to mono; the tyre path retains the removed panner's sqrt(1/2) centre gain.

The procedural engine uses asymmetric combustion pulses, short header reflections, crank-phase-aligned RPM/load layers and restrained idle variation. Off-load combustion is quieter. Recorded voices close their acoustic low-pass further under engine braking; their source recordings are unchanged. Turbo engines blend compressor airflow with their existing whistle. Hot petrol overrun is limited to a short lift window; diesel exhaust does not crackle. Existing shift cuts/clunks remain. Road roll adds a modest tyre-cavity resonance that follows road speed, independent of engine RPM.

No master/default/bus gain, compressor or loudness target is changed. Targets remain those in `assets/audio/MIX.md`. The deterministic 14-second comparison fixture stresses a full rev sweep, shift, lift, asymmetric slip and gravel; it is not the normal-race LUFS audit. Peak and channel measurements, before/after WAVs and spectrograms are in `.scratch/sound-v3/`. Original catalogue provenance and synthesized/recorded distinctions remain unchanged.

Reproduce in a browser with the project's Three import map: `renderSoundComparison(save, prefix)` and `runPanningProof(save)` from `tools/sound-v3-checks.js`. `save` accepts a filename and interleaved float32 ArrayBuffer; the returned rows include sample rate/channel metadata. Proof covers sources at +/-90 degrees in both camera modes, a 90-degree head turn, and each isolated player tyre. Node orientation tests: `node --test tools/audio-listener.test.mjs tools/audio-mix.test.mjs`. Existing engine suite: `tools/engine-audio-test.js`.
